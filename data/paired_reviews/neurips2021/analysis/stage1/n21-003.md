**Verdict.** The committees weighted the same facts differently, and only B's reviewers raised their scores after a near-identical rebuttal. Both saw the same strengths: 6.8x less attention memory than non-local attention (Table 5) and the best online results on YouTube-VIS and BDD100K. Both raised the same gaps: no frame-prototype count, missing details, no KITTI-MOTS results, slower than one-stage rivals, modest novelty. A also received BDD100K test results and a runtime comparison. No reviewer found an error in a result. A's reject rests on framing and on silence after the rebuttal. B's spotlight has no rating above 7 behind it. The PDF is the camera-ready (built 2021-11-08); it already holds the rebuttal's frame-prototype and KITTI-MOTS tables.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 5, 6, 5, 5; no review was edited. Reviewer_x3ez (confidence 5) asked the sharpest questions. The authors' own timing shows PCAN is not faster than non-local attention (63.2 vs 61.8 ms per frame). Table 5 shows a gain of only 0.5-0.6 AP. x3ez wrote "Most of my concerns are addressed" and asked for a fixed-memory comparison. The authors gave 35.4 AP at length 32 in 5.8 GB against 33.6 AP for non-local attention at length 8 in 9.6 GB. x3ez did not reply and stayed at 5. Reviewer_h7Rc called the work "a pure application paper" and YouTube-VIS a VOS dataset. Section 4.1 refutes the second claim, and h7Rc did not answer the correction. Reviewer_q7JA was right that MAST is not cited, the loss is not described, and all ablations use Table 1's validation split. Reviewer_nMFp's 6 rests on four one-line strengths; nMFp never posted again. The AC wrote "all reviewers were negative", which nMFp's unedited 6 contradicts. No public comment contains the AC's "low novelty and insight".

**Committee B (spotlight).** Ratings 7, 6, 7, 7. Reviewer_NPPQ ("I am not an expert in MOTS") "couldn't find a work that has done this before". Yet Section 2 cites EM-based prototype attention for single images [19], first-authored by this paper's second author. NPPQ also asked for an accuracy-loss ablation that Table 5 already gives, then moved from 6 to 7. Reviewer_HvVc called the paper "badly written" and raised to 7 on promised fixes. Reviewer_TiXB wrote the most careful review. TiXB still wanted KITTI-MOTS results but saw "a big step" and kept 7. Reviewer_9ARZ judged the work "marginal yet solid" and kept 6. The AC called the cost-accuracy trade-off "very strong". That holds for memory, not speed. B's speed table showed SG-Net (23 FPS) but left out STMask (28 FPS); PCAN runs at 15.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A checked Table 5's efficiency claim against wall-clock time, and the rebuttal confirms no speed gain. Only A asked for MAST and the contrastive random walk; neither is cited, even in the camera-ready. Only A flagged tuning on the reporting split. The rebuttal's BDD100K test result (31.8 vs 26.0 mMOTSA) answers that. Only h7Rc doubted that the method suits MOT, partly on a misreading. Only B checked the VisTR number: the submission used 34.4 AP from arXiv v1. The camera-ready uses 35.6, so PCAN's ResNet-50 lead is 0.5 AP. Only B found the Eq. 4 typo and the Fig. 3 error, both conceded and minor. A's points weaken the efficiency and novelty claims but leave the accuracy results standing.
