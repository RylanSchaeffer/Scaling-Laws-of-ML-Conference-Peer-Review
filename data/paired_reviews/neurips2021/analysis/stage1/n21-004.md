**Verdict.** A rejected because its two most thorough reviewers and its AC probed the evaluation, and the submission confirms their facts. B accepted because no B reviewer asked how the baselines were run. All models on APC used ten slots, though each APC image holds one object (l.278-279). Table 4 scores all pixels, so one object segment plus one background segment is optimal. Figure 6 shows GENESIS and Slot Attention splitting the APC background into many segments. APC carries the only large gain over Slot Attention (ARI 0.55 vs 0.03, one seed). On the synthetic sets GENESIS-v2 leads by 0.05 ARI-FG and trails on MSC-FG. Reviewer_KxNW asked for a smaller-K control; the authors did not run it, and A's AC called it "a crucial experiment". Both committees saw the weak flexible-K evidence and the merged background; B treated both as text fixes. The camera-ready replaces "outperforms the baseline methods by significant margins" with "performs strongly in comparison to", which supports A's reading.
