**Verdict.** Committee A rejected and Committee B accepted on mostly the same facts. α-IoU raises IoU losses to a power α (α = 3 in the main tables). This up-weights high-IoU boxes, as focal loss and GCE reweight classification losses. Gains over IoU baselines are consistent but small: +0.25 to +0.43 mAP on YOLOv5x (Table 1), with no error bars. Both committees saw the small gains and the emphasis on easy boxes. In A, the two confidence-5 reviewers counted them against the paper. B's reviewers judged them minor next to simplicity, breadth and robustness to noisy boxes. One fact reached only A. Reviewer_LYiS asked for other methods that favour high-IoU boxes. The authors' own rebuttal table then showed Dynamic R-CNN level on mAP (39.10 vs 38.96-39.43) and ahead at AP75 (42.80 vs at most 42.18). Neither AC added a reading of its own.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 4, 5, 4. No review was edited and no score moved. Reviewer_u1Yw made two correct, specific points. First, L155 says the power transform keeps the triangle inequality and cites only the GIoU paper. That is false for α < 1, which the family includes: boxes [0,1]², [0.5,1.5]×[0,1] and [1,2]×[0,1] break it at α = 0.5. The camera-ready keeps the sentence. Second, u1Yw asked whether the gain comes from a larger regression-loss weight against the fixed classification loss. Property 2 confirms that the loss grows by up to a factor α, and the main text has no control. The rebuttal said only that the classification loss was fixed. Reviewer_LYiS correctly found the gains shrinking on stronger detectors, but wrongly said Focal-EIoU was missing; Table 2 has it. Reviewer_vuNT (0.5 hours) found a real conflict in powering the penalty terms and promised to be satisfied if it was fixed. The authors ran α2 = 0.5, and vuNT never replied. The AC cited unanimity, Dynamic R-CNN and the GCE/focal-loss resemblance.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7. Reviewer_SBt3 and Reviewer_Zi6N raised u1Yw's doubt: the loss favours easy, high-IoU boxes. SBt3 also said that AP50 and mAP barely move. That is true for AP50, which changes by at most 0.43 points in Table 1. SBt3 gave 7 anyway and never edited the review. Zi6N opened with "a very mixed feeling", but called the paper "a great contribution" after a recall table with gains under one point. Reviewer_k9Mu raised its rating to 7 after the authors explained the gradient dynamics and the choice of α. Three reviews are thin. Reviewer_TBNp (confidence 2) wrote two lines and asked for Faster R-CNN, which Table 2 already has. Reviewer_LZaD (0.5 hours) listed no weakness. Reviewer_BMDv gave 7 with two plotting remarks. TBNp and LYiS got the same defence of the YOLOv5x result ("~0.6% in mAP", a relative figure of about 0.3 points). TBNp moved to accept; LYiS did not. The AC wrote two generic sentences.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A asked for comparisons with other methods that favour high-IoU boxes. Neither committee saw that Table 2 already puts Focal-EIoU ahead of every α variant at AP75 (42.40). Only A questioned novelty against GCE and focal loss; SBt3 called the idea "novel and interesting". Only A raised the unproven metric claim, the loss-weight confound and the missing baseline hyperparameters. Only B asked for FCOS, ATSS and stronger backbones. The rebuttal added FCOS (+0.88 mAP) and ResNet-101 Faster R-CNN (+0.78). Only B raised recall, training stability, the choice of α and the cause of the robustness to noise.
