**Verdict.** Both committees' reviewers favoured acceptance: A gave 7, 7, 7, 4 and B gave 7, 6, 7, 7. A's area chair rejected against three accept votes; B's area chair followed the scores. A's meta-review rests on two objections from the lone detractor, Reviewer_bubM. SILG uses partial environments (stage 1 of RTFM and Messenger, three NetHack tasks), and the game-to-interface mapping is unspecified. Both have a basis: the submission did not say which text feeds which model field, and the authors promised a table. But no other A reviewer treated either point as decisive. Reviewer_hUgP raised both, then kept 7; the hesitations left (SymTD, FiLM2 ablations) were "not crucial weaknesses". bubM wrote after the rebuttal that clarity "is no longer a major concern". B's reviewers saw related facts (unclear text fields in Figure 1, a simplified Touchdown, one human expert) and did not count them against the paper. The committees weighted the same facts differently; the difference sits with A's area chair.

**Committee A (reject).** bubM (13 h, confidence 5) wrote the only critical review and raised to 4. Most of its facts hold: stage-1 curricula, per-environment hyperparameters, separate training, and Base and Best overlapping in four of six Table 2 columns. One does not: 23 actions is the action set of NLE's own tasks, not a cut from 93. bubM stayed unconvinced only on what simplified, separately trained environments add. Reviewer_tPsp (7) said the paper gave enough to build the model. Reviewer_zwvr (7) wrote a thin review and said there was no impact statement, although hUgP and tPsp found one in the appendix. The area chair went beyond bubM: details are "completely missing", and reviewers "could not evaluate the technical correctness". The meta-review recasts the authors' reason for omitting later stages as poor numbers; the authors cited curriculum learning and presentation. Its fear of an "unplayable" mapping is answered by expert win rates of 100% on RTFM, Messenger and ALFWorld and 78.1% on NetHack. Only SymTD (61.5%) supports it, and hUgP raised that while keeping 7. "None of us could understand" implies a private discussion that the record does not show.

**Committee B (poster).** No detractor. Reviewer_UhjS (4 h) wrote the most careful review and raised 6 to 7. Its checkable points hold: SIR trails SOTA on Messenger (31 vs 85) and ALFWorld, VisTD uses only 10-dimensional PCA features, and BERT helps only on Messenger. UhjS accepted the claim that components are environment-specific only as motivation. Reviewer_gUh8 (2 h) wrote generic praise and raised to 7, citing the promised public release. Reviewer_h3yz questioned the exclusion of perception; Reviewer_MASS (6) flagged unconverged curves and separate training. No reviewer replied in the thread. The meta-review says the reviewers "all advocate for acceptance" and asks for clearer reasons for the environment choice.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A raised the partial curricula, the first reason A's area chair gave. Both saw the unstated text-field roles: bubM and hUgP called them a reproducibility gap and A's area chair made them a reason to reject, while UhjS listed them as a presentation suggestion. Only A questioned per-environment hyperparameters, the SymTD conversion and the missing FiLM2 ablation. Only B questioned imitation learning, the auto-stop Touchdown setup, "reasonable performance across all environments" given the Messenger gap, and the weak VisTD baseline. None of B's points concerns completeness. Both saw separate per-environment training and unfinished curves in Figures 6 and 7.
