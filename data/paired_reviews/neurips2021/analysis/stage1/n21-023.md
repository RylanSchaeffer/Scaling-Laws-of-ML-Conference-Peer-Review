**Verdict**

The spotlight/poster split is score calibration, not substance. Both committees saw a sound, clearly written extension of Marteau-Ferey et al. (Tikhonov regularization with generalized self-concordant losses) to iterated Tikhonov, which removes saturation and reaches optimal rates. Both noted modest novelty and dense notation. Neither found a flaw in the results. Three A reviewers gave 7 and three B reviewers gave 8 for similar assessments, so the means were 7.25 and 7.75. Each AC wrote a short meta-review that follows its mean and gives no reason for the tier. The visible weighting difference is novelty. In A, Reviewer_rN14 called the gain "somewhat incremental", and the AC's only specific request is the Marteau-Ferey comparison. In B, Reviewer_r7mh made the same observation, called it a small issue, and framed the result as the answer to an open question from Marteau-Ferey et al.; B's AC used the same framing.

**Committees**

Committee A (7, 7, 7, 8; poster). Reviewer_CRtT (10 h) wrote the most careful review. It asked how Theorem 1 behaves as t grows, found a supplement/main-text mismatch in the capacity condition, and questioned Tikhonov's qualification of 1/2. All three points were valid, and the camera-ready addresses each. The authors answered the qualification point with a notation argument that they later withdrew in committee B. CRtT never replied, so A's record keeps the wrong answer. Reviewer_xAhK is thin (3 h, one question, no discussion), though its question on the unbounded logistic minimizer is sound. Reviewer_rN14 raised its score to 7 after the authors gave an explicit rate comparison with Marteau-Ferey. Reviewer_Kz6Q, posted 18 days after the others, gave the only 8 with a substantive review. The three-sentence meta-review states consensus and asks for the Marteau-Ferey comparison.

Committee B (8, 8, 8, 7; spotlight). Reviewer_7njB (8 h) gave the best-supported 8: detailed notation fixes, a link to sequential greedy approximation, and an explicit statement that the contributions are significant. Reviewer_r7mh wrote a thoughtful review, but at confidence 2. Reviewer_KRE1's 8 at confidence 5 rests on a six-sentence main review of praise; its only reservations, in the limitations field, are minor. It shows no sign of the math check that confidence 5 claims, and it did not see the qualification error that CRtT and v7Aq found. Reviewer_v7Aq began at "weak reject" over that error, pressed the authors twice, got a concession that it was a typo outside the proofs, and moved to 7. The AC summarized the theorems and stressed broad interest in kernel methods. Note: a/paper.pdf is the camera-ready, not the submission. Its p.5 now says "qualification 1", but the same paragraph still gives a wrong saturated rate for Tikhonov (n^{-α/(1+α)}), which contradicts Theorem 1 and Table 1.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A asked for:
- an explicit rate comparison with Marteau-Ferey et al. (rN14);
- the t-dependence of Theorem 1's constants, and how to choose t (CRtT, Kz6Q);
- the effect of the output dimension (Kz6Q);
- other definitions of self-concordance and a ball-oracle reference (rN14).

Only B raised the cost of Newton-based inner solvers (v7Aq), the unknown dependence on the confidence level (KRE1), the sequential greedy approximation literature (7njB), and the missing intuition for the source and capacity conditions (r7mh). None of these is a flaw, and none explains the tier. The shared issues (the qualification error, incrementality, the assumption that the minimizer lies in H) weighed more in A only through rN14's score.
