**Verdict.** The committees weighted the same facts differently, and only A had a reviewer who tested the novelty claim. Both saw that CoPE is close to Π-Net and that no experiment compares it with state-of-the-art conditional models. In A, Reviewer_41qE showed that Eq. 2 is Π-Net's recursion on the concatenated input, with a partitioned weight matrix. The authors conceded that the two "could learn a similar model" and that the differences are design choices. Reviewer_Mncu found bolded gains inside the run-to-run spread, and Tables 3 and 4 confirm it for CIFAR10. A's three low scores never moved, and its one 7 came from a two-hour review. B read the same closeness as "a natural continuation" (tbWn) or "significant novelty" (Rugm). B's concerns were requests for details, metrics and comparisons, which a rebuttal can answer; three B reviewers raised. Nobody found an error in a result. A underweighted the one large gain: Cars196 FID 55.5 against 120.4 for Π-Net (Table 4). No submitted PDF is in the folder. The checks use the camera-ready (built 2022-01-21); its table values match those quoted in the reviews and rebuttals.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 4, 7, 5, 4. Reviewer_41qE (12 hours) wrote the strongest review. It noted that the rebuttal's gains are over the weak Π-Net-SICONC: the "~18% improvement" is the IS gain over it (7.87 vs 6.65). Against Π-Net the IS gain is 4%, and FID is 34.35 ± 2.68 vs 37.26 ± 1.86; the bands overlap. The authors also conceded that products of polynomials cannot represent every polynomial and that gradients are not bounded. Reviewer_oDAB misread Table 3: 21.74 is SNGAN-SPADE's FID, not SNGAN's. Its main points hold: no state-of-the-art baselines, low resolution, and a small gain over plain SNGAN (14.22 vs 14.70). Reviewer_La4a (7, confidence 3) gave generic praise, listed only a typo as a weakness, and never commented. The AC saw "no one strongly in favor" and followed the three detractors. The meta-review ignores the rebuttal and the Cars196 result.

**Committee B (spotlight).** Ratings 7, 8, 7, 6. Reviewer_tbWn, Reviewer_Rugm and Reviewer_kHDT raised their scores; the meta-review says one did. Rugm found CoPE "quite similar" to Π-Net but accepted the authors' list of differences. Reviewer_7vAs ("not an expert", three hours) gave two sentences of praise and two clarity questions. Its "clear improvements over baselines" fails for CIFAR10. kHDT raised doubts close to A's: no state-of-the-art baselines, Table 4 baselines at FID 294+, and FID alone for super-resolution. kHDT raised to 6 on "the tables" in the rebuttal. There, the "Π-net" super-resolution row (SSIM 0.31, LPIPS 0.200, FID 186.42) is Π-Net-SICONC in Table 5. The "BigGAN" row has no IS, and its FID equals SNGAN's in Table 3 (14.70). The meta-review says all reviewers found the work novel; kHDT called it "a straightforward follow-up". It gives no reason for a spotlight.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A tested whether CoPE differs from Π-Net in substance; the authors conceded a similar model and design-choice differences. Only A asked for an ablation of those choices. Only A checked bolded gains against the standard deviations. Only A questioned the product of polynomials and the stability measures. Only B asked for SSIM or PSNR, a toy test with a known distribution, and the published CIFAR10 results of Karras et al. (2020). Only B found the Appendix D typo. Both noted that Π-Net is missing from super-resolution (41qE in A; Rugm in B, via Tables 4 and 5). A's points weaken the novelty claim and the CIFAR10 results. They leave the Cars196 and super-resolution gains over SPADE and Π-Net-SICONC standing.
