**Verdict.** Both committees accepted. The spotlight-versus-poster split is area-chair discretion applied to two records of very different depth. Committee A ended at four 7s. Three were raises after the rebuttal, two of them on baseline numbers the authors copied from other papers. One of the four was a confidence-1, 1.5-hour review. A's chair made this uniform-7 slate a spotlight with a two-sentence meta-review that cites only consensus. No A reviewer rated above 7, and two doubted the significance or the adoption of the method. Committee B spent about 26 reviewer-hours to A's 10.5. It uncovered an undisclosed data-loader bug that color-inverted the CIFAR-10-C images behind Fig. 6. It also pressed the motivation for the DSCS kernel until the authors' own ablation showed that a quadratic-variance arc-cosine kernel nearly matches it (FPR@95 26.2 vs 24.2 on CIFAR-10, 62.5 vs 63.0 on CIFAR-100). Two B reviewers stayed at 6, and B's chair kept the paper at poster. B held the only 8 in either committee, so ratings alone do not explain the split. B knew more about the paper's weaknesses; A's chair was more generous with a thinner record.

**Committee A (spotlight).** Ratings 7, 7, 7, 7; confidences 3, 1, 3, 4. Reviewer_z1y4 raised to 7 "given the comparison with Mahalanobis", numbers taken from Meinke and Hein's Table 2. Reviewer_h4G7 raised to 7 for a DUQ comparison copied from the DUQ paper, where RGPR only ties DUQ on AUROC (92.6 vs 92.7). The meta-review praises the problem, the writing and the support, and gives no reason for a spotlight.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 6, 7, 8, 6; confidences 3, 4, 4, 2. Reviewer_oTxs asked why CIFAR-10-C accuracy was so low. Reviewer_YyKC asked why last-layer Laplace beat Deep Ensembles. The authors first described an extra "color inversion" in the setup. Pressed by YyKC, they admitted a loader bug they had not known about. In the rerun, RGPR barely improves on LLL (NLL 0.872 to 0.869, Brier unchanged, ECE worse with the OOD objective). oTxs raised to 8 and YyKC to 6. Reviewer_4z2v (confidence 2, but 8 hours and a long follow-up) kept 6: the infinite-ReLU framing and the DSCS choice stayed unmotivated. The meta-review cites motivation and the last-layer-Laplace-only experiments. It omits the bug and calls the paper "practically sound".

**Issues raised by one committee only.** Only B raised:

- the CIFAR-10-C loader bug;
- a weak Deep Ensemble baseline (Table 1 still has DE worse than a single MAP net at FPR@95 on CIFAR-10, 51.0 vs 38.9, and no reviewer queried it);
- the ablation that separates the practical gains from the cubic-growth theory;
- missing Outlier Exposure and Hein et al. baselines;
- regression, and a non-asymptotic version of the main theorem.

Only A raised Mahalanobis, DUQ and SNGP baselines, unquantified approximation errors, "BNN" used for any Gaussian around a solution, and the layer-independence assumption in Eq. (10). Both raised the quadratic-versus-cubic argument stated before it is explained, last-layer Laplace as the only main-text BNN, per-layer tuning, and the near-origin assumption.
