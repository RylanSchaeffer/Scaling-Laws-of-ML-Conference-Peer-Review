**Verdict.** The committees weighted the same facts differently, and the gap predates the rebuttal. Both saw one method and one Table 2. There ASSLN beats IMDN by 0.08 to 0.39 dB at nearly equal size (692K vs 694K parameters at ×2). B's reviewers called this "strong" and "obviously better". A's only positive reviewer called it "marginal". A's two 4s were not revised after the rebuttal, and B's Reviewer_DsQ3 kept its initial 8. The rebuttal then widened the gap: two B reviewers rose, and A's Reviewer_k8sL fell to 5. One fact reached only A. The authors' own table for Reviewer_1kmf puts LatticeNet (ECCV 2020) within 0.04 dB of ASSLN at ×4. One fact reached only B. DsQ3 named prior SR pruning work (DHP; an ICASSP 2020 paper) that contradicts the paper's "first attempt" claim, and B did not weigh it. No reviewer found an error in a result. The PDF is the camera-ready (built 2022-01-21); no submitted version is in the folder, but its Table 2 matches the rebuttal tables.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 5, 4, 4. Reviewer_k8sL (1 hour, confidence 5) wrote a three-sentence main review: "well-written", "experiments are adequate". The authors answered its three requests (EDSR+KD, runtime, the soft SSA penalty). k8sL then agreed with the others that novelty "is not highlighted enough" and that gains are "marginal (compared with LatticeNet and IMDN)", and lowered the score to 5. The LatticeNet half is correct. Reviewer_XysC (confidence 3) asked framing questions and for RCAN and IPT results. Reviewer_1kmf called novelty "limited" on three references. None of them prunes an SR network, and one is a 2009 paper on kernel adaptive filters. Neither 4 replied to the rebuttal, which added RCAN+ASSL results and three new baselines for them. The AC closely paraphrased k8sL's two points.

**Committee B (spotlight).** Ratings 8, 8, 8, all at confidence 5. Reviewer_DsQ3 (15 hours) wrote the most careful review and kept 8. DsQ3 accepted the authors' list of differences from DHP and the ICASSP paper. Reviewer_Lh3t asked for runtime. The answer shows parity with IMDN (51.0 vs 51.8 ms), not a speedup, yet Lh3t raised the score. Reviewer_chSU (2 hours) praised the "first attempt" claim. The authors' own reply to DsQ3 confirms that the ICASSP paper pruned SRResNet and LapSRN. chSU had promised a raise for answers, and the weight-normalization ablation and sensitivity tables satisfied it. The AC's four sentences praise the paper, cite the reviewers' positivity and give no other reason.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A compared against 2020 lightweight SR networks (LatticeNet, MADNet, s-LWSR); the camera-ready Table 2 still omits them. Only A noted that the SSA penalty does not guarantee aligned masks; the authors conceded. Only A asked for RCAN and IPT. The RCAN+ASSL table gives no model size and shows the pruned model beating full RCAN on every set; nobody probed it. Only B raised prior SR pruning work, HRank, weight normalization, hyper-parameter sensitivity and the uniform pruning ratio. B's added tables support the method: weight normalization alone adds nothing, and ASSL beats HRank by 0.03 to 0.34 dB. Both copies show small margins over L1-norm pruning in Table 1 (0.03 to 0.12 dB up to ratio 0.7). Only A's AC called the gains over pruning methods "fairly small".
