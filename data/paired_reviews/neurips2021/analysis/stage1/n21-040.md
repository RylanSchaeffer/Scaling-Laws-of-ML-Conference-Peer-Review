**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster for the same reasons. Each had three reviewers at 6 or above and one 4 that did not move. Each AC followed the majority. Both majorities relied on the same evidence: VisDA (85.9 against SHOT's 82.9), PointDA-10 (52.6 against PointDAN's 48.7) and clear ablations. The authors posted one runtime table in both forums, and it settled the scalability concern. Both committees saw the same weakness: small gains on Office-31 (0.8 over SHOT, 0.2 below Model Adaptation) and Office-Home (0.4). Neither treated it as decisive. Each detractor had a specific reason that the record supports, and each AC overruled it. B's higher mean (6.0 against 5.5) carries little information. Reviewer_tPBC's 7 comes from a two-hour review with generic praise. In A, Reviewer_4Ef6 entered its 6 after the authors asked for a 6. The ACs differed only in tone on novelty: A found it "interesting", B saw an "incremental flavor". The same facts and the same score profile gave the same decision.

**Committees**

Committee A (6, 6, 4, 6). Reviewer_ERRY and Reviewer_LSVJ wrote careful 6s. ERRY found a real error: Eq. 7 takes a KL divergence between scalars. Reviewer_eN4k (4, confidence 5) is a principled detractor, and the paper confirms the main points. K and M change per dataset (l.202-204), and no selection rule is given. The K-M grid on VisDA (Fig. 4) spans 83.4 to 85.9, most of the margin over SHOT. Table 4 has no source-free baseline. eN4k disputed all four rebuttal answers and did not reply to the later validation-split experiment. Reviewer_4Ef6 (2 hours, confidence 5) cited Pan et al. 2020 against the novelty claim. The paper's l.276-277 does overclaim, and its own related work cites clustering methods. 4Ef6 accepted the local-against-global argument. On 2021-08-31 the authors asked for a 6, and 4Ef6 answered "The score is corrected." The AC counted three leaning to accept and agreed with them.

Committee B (7, 4, 6, 7). Reviewer_meMo (7) wrote the most detailed review and caught that l.147 calls a vector a scalar. meMo offered to raise the score but never replied to the rebuttal. Reviewer_tPBC (7, 2 hours) gave generic strengths and three conceptual questions. The AC copied four sentences of tPBC's summary word for word, typos included. Reviewer_R8cb rose to 6 after new ablations, for example 76.3 without the diversity loss. In return R8cb asked for a limitations section; the camera-ready has none. Reviewer_4Suf (4, 35 hours) could not reproduce the results with the submitted code. The authors admitted that the code "accidentally omitted some functionality". The new code covers VisDA and PointDA-10 only. 4Suf kept the 4 and gave no reason. 4Suf also asked for a pre-adaptation t-SNE that Fig. 1(a) already shows. The meta-review does not mention reproducibility. It names a novelty concern that no public B review raises.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A challenged novelty against clustering-based adaptation and the Section 5 claim (4Ef6). Only A asked how to choose K and M without target labels (eN4k, ERRY). Only A raised the Eq. 7 error, the clean clusters in Fig. 1(c), and DomainNet. Only B raised these issues:

- the submitted code that did not reproduce the results (4Suf);
- the l.214-215 claim that source-free methods beat source-present ones, which Table 1 contradicts on Office-31 (R8cb);
- one affinity, 0.1, for both nRNNs and expanded neighbors (R8cb, 4Suf);
- the diversity loss under class imbalance (R8cb);

None of these changed a decision. The camera-ready keeps Eq. 7 and the l.214-215 claim, and it adds no limitations section.
