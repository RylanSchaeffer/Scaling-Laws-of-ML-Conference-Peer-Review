**Verdict.** The two committees weighted the same facts differently. No reviewer on either side found an error. Both committees saw the first reward-free results for linear mixture MDPs, built from known parts: the exploration-driven reward of Wang et al. [17], value-targeted regression, and the Bernstein bonus of [28]. Both noted the loose lower bound and the hard optimisation behind the d² rate. Each committee got two score raises after the rebuttal. In B, both raises crossed the threshold. In A, Reviewer_GGZY stopped at 5, and Reviewer_G5QY (confidence 5) never moved. A's area chair (AC) then rejected because no reviewer would champion the paper and the paper never explained its technical challenges. B had no champion either, but its AC accepted because the paper "fills a gap in the literature". The paper bears out A's doubt. Remark 4.1 gives one difference from [17], the regression target, and calls it "mainly due to the different problem settings".

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 5, 6, 6, 5; the 5s carry confidence 4 and 5, the 6s confidence 3. G5QY asked whether the method is "a direct combination" of [17] and known linear-mixture tools. The authors answered with the pseudo value function and an extra regression. G5QY did not reply. GGZY rose from at most 4 to 5 on a promised comparison with [17], but stayed "not convinced on significance". Its sharpest point checks out. Section 4.3 calls problem (4.3) "hard". Corollaries 4.4 and 5.3 give the efficient relaxation Õ(H⁵d³ε⁻²), the same d³ as [17] and FRANCIS. The two 6s add little. Reviewer_ja4k wrote a thin review about time-homogeneity. Reviewer_a98J wrote careful line-level comments, also asked about [17], and stayed silent after the rebuttal. The AC called the missing technical challenges "quite important points" for a theory paper. The camera-ready main text adds no such section and keeps Remark 4.1 unchanged.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 6, 6, 6, 6, softer than they look. Reviewer_6DWQ (10 hours) still writes "I choose the weak reject" beside its 6. It edited the review on 2021-08-31, one day after the authors asked it to "reconsider the rating", and posted no comment. Reviewer_Zh4L called the work "a relatively simple combination of existing ideas". It raised its score on 2021-09-13 after two author follow-ups and gave only "the discussion and re-reading" as its reason. The second follow-up said "the other reviewers are all satisfied". That was an overstatement: Reviewer_USFu and Reviewer_nnra had only kept their 6s. nnra's review is thin; its first point is the title. USFu's review is balanced, and its facts check out. It calls the paper "a borderline accept". The AC followed the scores.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A asked for a section on technical challenges against [17] (G5QY, AC). Only A tied the d improvement to the intractable step (GGZY). USFu reported the d³ relaxed rate but still called the d² bound the state of the art. Only A raised time-homogeneity (ja4k), Table 1 errors (G5QY on FRANCIS, a98J on tabular settings) and proof-level corrections (a98J). Only B argued that the true lower bound is of order d² (6DWQ). The paper does not settle that, and the rebuttal's best-arm example does not settle it either. 6DWQ's corollary that "the dependence is always quadratic" is wrong: for H ≥ d, Remark 5.2 gives Õ(H⁵dε⁻²). Only B flagged the "more general" claim in Remark 3.2 (6DWQ), missing citations (USFu, 6DWQ) and the title (nnra).
