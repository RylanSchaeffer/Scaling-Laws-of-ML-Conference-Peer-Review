**Verdict.** Committee B accepted on three lukewarm 6s (mean 6.0). Committee A rejected on 6, 5, 7, 3 (mean 5.25). Both committees saw the same facts. The paper is theory only. A standard Laplace channel feeds a standard CUSUM-type procedure, and the contribution is near-matching private upper and lower bounds. There are no experiments, tuning needs known model constants, and N_h is exponential in d. B's Reviewer_P1kk listed most of these weaknesses and still gave 6 for the lower bound. A's Reviewer_KJKZ treated the same weaknesses as decisive and gave 5. A also drew Reviewer_dv2E, whose 3 rests on a wrong correctness objection. Without dv2E, A's mean is 6.0, equal to B's. A's two positive reviews were thin, so they gave the AC little to set against the negatives. A's AC called a 2–2 split a consensus that the paper "just misses the bar" and gave no reason. The split comes from the reviewer draw and from different weights on practicality, not from a flaw that only one committee found.

**Committee A (reject).** Reviewer_dv2E (3, confidence 4) doubted the guarantee P∞(t̂ < ∞) < γ because "any change point detection algorithms will stop sooner or later". That holds for fixed thresholds. The thresholds in eq. (3) and (9) grow with t, so a union bound over all (s, t) gives overall control, as in the paper's reference [33]. The authors explained this on 2021-08-10; dv2E never replied or edited. dv2E's other points (CUSUM naming, Assumption 4, motivation, experiments) ask for clarification and do not support "clear rejection". Reviewer_KJKZ (5) wrote the most careful A review. KJKZ was right that Theorem 1 needs M0, σ, C_Lip and κ, and that N_h is exponential in d. After the rebuttal KJKZ kept 5: the paper gives no "tools that we might use in the real world". Reviewer_ALYo (7) offers generic praise ("a very high quality paper") and no specifics. Reviewer_djMH (6, confidence 2) worked from "superficial skimming of the proofs". The meta-review copies dv2E's summary and says "the reviewers agree"; they did not.

**Committee B (accept).** Reviewers gcFi, P1kk and FYQ4 each gave 6 after short reviews of two to three hours. All three placed the contribution in the near-matching bounds. P1kk and FYQ4 called the privacy mechanism and the detection method standard; the paper agrees (lines 98–101, 111). They were right that about three-quarters of page 9 is empty, that the main text has no lower-bound proof sketch, and that the univariate case sits in the supplement. P1kk also noted the known-parameter requirement and the high computation cost, the facts that moved KJKZ to 5. No B reviewer moved after the rebuttal. The AC accepted on unanimity and asked for a better presentation.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A questioned whether overall false-alarm control is possible (dv2E, wrongly). Only A raised missing work on LDP tracking of evolving data and a lack of new insight about LDP (KJKZ). Only A doubted that data-driven tuning fits a local-privacy setting (KJKZ). Only B asked for a lower-bound proof sketch, use of the empty page, the order of C_ε, and whether the assumptions serve privacy or utility. Both committees noted the missing experiments, the standard components, the known-parameter requirement, the computation cost and the univariate case in the supplement.
