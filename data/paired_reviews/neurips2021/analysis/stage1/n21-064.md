**Verdict.** Committee B accepted and Committee A rejected mainly because only B received a rebuttal. Both committees found the same weaknesses: a teacher retrained for each known student, unreported training cost, small ImageNet gains, hand-designed branches, and overlap with auxiliary-branch and online-distillation work. A's forum has no author response and no discussion. Its 5, 5, 4, 4 stood, and its chair added up individually minor concerns into a reject. In B the authors answered every reviewer with training times, similarity and agreement tables, an entropy analysis and a requested CIFAR-100-C experiment. Reviewer_6fou moved from "cannot recommend acceptance" to 7, Reviewer_o6Fn moved up to 5, and B's chair credited the "heroic effort". B also started warmer, with two 6s, but both are thin. The committees also weighted novelty differently: A counted the overlap with deep supervision against the paper, and B treated it as a citation to add.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 5, 5, 4, 4, all at confidence 4. Both edits predate the authors' responses in B. Reviewer_9tKz's points hold: ImageNet has one ResNet50/ResNet34 pair with top-1 gains of +0.38 to +0.59 (Table 3), and no training time is reported. 9tKz offered to raise the score if answered; no answer came. Reviewer_Bfa6's requested baseline, an independent jointly trained student before the KD stage, is absent. But Bfa6's novelty case ignores Table 4, where SFTN beats DML and KDCL in all six pairs. Reviewer_rkNh asked for a study of students that differ from the branch; Table 7 and Section 4.5 are that study. rkNh's reading of resnet32x4 as a ResNeXt is wrong. Reviewer_6tca's 4 rests on "not particularly novel nor exciting", with no prior work named. The meta-review names no reviewer and no flaw.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 6, 7, 5, 6. Reviewer_6fou wrote the most specific review in either committee, and its checkable points hold. Eq. (1) and (4), read literally, temper the teacher's cross-entropy. The main paper has no error bars, though checklist item 3(c) says "Yes". The authors conceded the notation and moved 6fou with new analyses. Those analyses are modest: the CIFAR-100-C margin over KD falls from +1.08 at the mildest corruption to +0.18 at the strongest, and SFTN trails KD in 6 of 20 cells. Error bars and the Inception citation were only promised, and the camera-ready main text has neither. Reviewer_o6Fn (confidence 5) rose only to 5. Its two main points, dependence on the student and small ImageNet gains, are correct (L244-246, Table 3). Reviewer_sdwv (confidence 3, "less familiar") asked for the teacher-student distance analysis that Table 9 reports, and never returned. Reviewer_2smh judged the paper partly against its "ICML rejection", asserted an unsupported O(n log n) overhead, and kept 6 in one line. The meta-review paraphrases 6fou.

**Issues raised by one committee only.** Only A raised:

- novelty as a reason to reject (Bfa6, rkNh, 6tca);
- large-gap and heterogeneous ImageNet pairs (9tKz);
- a two-stage independent-student baseline and an equal-compute teacher (Bfa6);
- similarity measured only on the CIFAR-100 test set (6tca);
- the need for the branch cross-entropy and for multiple branches (6tca).

Only B raised the temperature notation, missing error bars, unnormalized loss weights and dataset shift (all 6fou), and scope beyond supervised vision (2smh, sdwv). Both raised training cost, dependence on a known student, branch design, small ImageNet gains, and why SFTN works.
