**Verdict.** The committees saw largely the same facts and weighed them differently. Committee B's four reviewers all gave 7, and B's chair restated the scores. Committee A's ratings were 3, 6, 6, 7 (mean 5.5), and no rating moved after the rebuttal. A's chair rejected against the three accept-side ratings, on four open questions. Two apply to any attribution paper: how to show that one explanation beats another, and whether the Shapley axioms suit the task. The paper itself concedes the other two: remote test points (L143-146) and Euclidean distance in high dimension (L242-244, L312-315). The evaluation doubt has support. Fig. 5, the main fidelity test, weights errors with the same kind of exponential kernel that the method fits. B's reviewers saw the same gap and accepted a one-dataset ROAR table as the fix. A's reviewers also found real errors that B missed: Proposition 1 calls a self-normalised estimator unbiased (L114-116), and the Theorem 2 proof had algebra errors. The authors conceded both, and A's chair cited neither. So the split is chair discretion on a borderline paper, not a fatal flaw that one committee caught.

**Committee A (reject).** Reviewer_iiTd (3, confidence 5, 1.5 hours) holds that local baselines hide how the model uses features off the manifold. That is a contested stance, and the paper argues the other side with citations (L123-135). The checkable claims are trivial or wrong. The P(S) weight at L49 lacks a "-1", a typo. The Shapley axioms hold for any value function, so the missing proof is a presentation gap. Neighbourhood SHAP is not a special case of [11], a global importance measure. Figs. 4-5 report quantitative metrics, so evaluation is not "mainly based on human intuition". iiTd never answered the rebuttal. Reviewer_dkVj (6, confidence 2) found the Proposition 1 error. Reviewer_6nZp (6) found the Theorem 2 errors and asked for harder data. Reviewer_xvf9's 7 rests on four generic lines of praise. In the visible record, no reviewer argued for acceptance after the rebuttal. The meta-review answers none of the rebuttal's points. It even asks for a caveat "in the final version of this work".
