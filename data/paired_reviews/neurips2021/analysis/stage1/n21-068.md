**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster for the same reasons. Every reviewer liked the method, and six of seven called it novel. By the end, no reviewer doubted the large MSE gains of Table 1. No final rating is below 6 or above 7, so neither committee had a champion or a detractor. In each committee, two reviewers rose after a rebuttal full of new author-run experiments. A's mean is half a point higher (6.5 against 6.0) because of two 7s; the gap does not cross a tier. The committees differed in what they examined, not in the result: A examined the evaluation design, B the notation and baselines. Neither could check the rebuttal's numbers, and some changed later. In the camera-ready univariate table, N-BEATS (0.156) and ARIMA (0.112) beat Autoformer (0.241) on Exchange predict-96. The rebuttal table that A saw had Autoformer best there (0.126 against 0.319 and 0.308).
