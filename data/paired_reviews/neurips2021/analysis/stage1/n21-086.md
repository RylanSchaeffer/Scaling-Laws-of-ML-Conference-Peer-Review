**Verdict.** The two committees saw the same facts and put the paper on opposite sides of the threshold. A gave 5, 3, 5, 5; B gave 6, 7, 6, 6. Both found that the memory tokens are the one new component. The rest comes from DETR, VisTR, CondInst and MaskProp. Both found strong accuracy at high speed. Both learned in the rebuttal that Table 2a reports the best of five runs. A weighted the narrow novelty; B weighted the results. A's 3 (Reviewer_tpaP) does not decide the outcome: the other three A reviewers average 5.0. Two of B's 6s rest on thin reviews. The paper confirms the specific doubts in both committees. IFC adds 0.5 to 1.6 AP over no communication (Table 3a). Mask-based matching adds 2.2 AP (Table 2c). The camera-ready means lower every headline number by 1.4 to 2.0 AP. They leave the ResNet-101 model 0.1 AP above MaskProp (42.6 against 42.5).

**Committee A (reject).** Reviewer_qqgE (5) made the sharpest checkable point: the largest ablation gain comes from the matching, not the memory tokens. Reviewer_pxiA (5, confidence 5) traced each component to prior work. Yet its text reads above threshold: the work has strong points that "need to be shared with the community". pxiA edited the review on 2021-08-29, after the rebuttal. The AC says one reviewer lowered the score and describes an "initially more positive" reviewer; pxiA fits both. Reviewer_1red (5) found the 41.0 versus 39.0 AP gap that the paper does not explain. The rebuttal disclosed the best-of-five selection. Reviewer_tpaP (3, confidence 5) said IFC applies MaX-DeepLab's memory to video. The paper says so too (lines 80-84), but Gather-Communicate across frames is new. tpaP also erred: the mask head is not DETR's, and the COCO result it asked for is in Table 3b. No A reviewer posted after the rebuttal. The AC followed the scores and the novelty critique. It did not mention the best-of-five reporting.

**Committee B (poster).** Reviewer_8afQ (7, confidence 5, 8 hours) was the only substantive champion. It made the same two empirical points as A: the 41.0 versus 39 AP gap, and the shrinking gain of IFC over No Comm at larger clip lengths. Both are correct. After the rebuttal it called best-of-five reporting "a bad practice" but kept the 7 on condition of a fix. Reviewer_rHZQ (6) wrote a two-line main review. Reviewer_Z1DA (6) asked only clarifying questions, some answered in the paper, and gave no reason for the score. Reviewer_G3h2 (6) wrote "The novelty is limited" and still scored above threshold. No B score moved. The AC accepted on the assumption that averages "still outperform SOTA, albeit less significantly". The camera-ready confirms this, by 1.2 AP for ResNet-50 and 0.1 AP for ResNet-101.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A tied the encoder to MaX-DeepLab and called the FPS comparison misleading. Only A doubted the memory-token visualisation and asked about long-term tracking, TransVOD and MaskProp's speed. Only B asked for training and inference details. Only B asked why IFC is needed when decoder cross-attention already links frames. Only B noted the missing qualitative comparisons and contribution summary. None of these drove either decision. The difference is a threshold, not an issue. Both committees knew the method is incremental and its reported margin inflated. A called that marginally below the bar; B called it marginally above.
