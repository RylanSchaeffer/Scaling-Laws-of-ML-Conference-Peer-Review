## Verdict

Both committees recommended a poster for the same reasons, and they weighted the same facts the same way. Each had a majority at 6 or above, one confident 5 for limited novelty (Reviewer_vaM6 in A, Reviewer_Lf3c in B), and an area chair (AC) who followed the majority. No reviewer questioned the numbers in Table 1, where DGCL is best on all nine datasets. A reached its 6.0 mean with four close scores, and no reviewer answered the rebuttal. B's 6.33 rests on an uncritical 8 from Reviewer_JYuN and on Reviewer_T4kE's rise to 6 after new β-VAE and FactorVAE baselines. The main difference is scrutiny of the central claim. Only B examined Figure 4, the paper's one direct evidence of disentanglement. T4kE saw, correctly, that several channel blocks in Figure 4(c) correlate at about 0.6 to 0.8, more than GraphCL's features in 4(b). The authors did not answer this, and T4kE raised the score anyway. Neither committee questioned the submitted ogbg-molhiv result: 83.5 ROC-AUC from a logistic regression on self-supervised embeddings, above the best leaderboard entry the paper cites (82.3). The camera-ready replaced it with a fine-tuning experiment that reports 69.0 to 74.6.

## Committees

Committee A (6, 7, 6, 5). Reviewer_9Pj4 (7) wrote the most specific review: SVM-only evaluation, no fine-tuning, no factor analysis on real data, and nothing that forces exclusive factors. The paper confirms the last point; lines 218-225 claim disentanglement "without adding extra regularization term". Reviewer_ubo5 (6) correctly noted that no code came with the paper, although checklist item 3(a) says yes. Reviewer_mcrT (6) asked for parameter counts, runtimes and a comparison with FactorGCN. Reviewer_vaM6 (5) gave a novelty objection that names no prior module. vaM6 also asked for an unsupervised GIN ablation, which Table 2's K = 1 variant already is. The authors posted new tables, some unfavourable: after fine-tuning, DGCL trails InfoGraph on RDT-M5K. No reviewer replied. The AC "will follow the majority vote".

Committee B (5, 6, 8). Reviewer_JYuN (8, confidence 5, 15 hours) repeats the paper's own sentences (lines 61-63, 186, 197-198). It calls a standard variational bound "rigorous theoretical contributions" and asks only for a typo fix and kernel descriptions. That is not evidence of quality. Reviewer_T4kE (6) did the checking. New results settled its "biggest concern", the missing β-VAE and FactorVAE baselines; its Figure 4 and EM questions stayed open. Reviewer_Lf3c (5) objected to novelty and to generic GraphCL augmentations that may change the factors. Lf3c offered a 6 if the others were positive but never edited the score. The AC cited a consensus that "moved towards acceptance".

## Issues raised by one committee only

Only A: SVM-only evaluation and no fine-tuning (9Pj4); missing code (ubo5); no complexity or runtime figures (vaM6, mcrT); FactorGCN (mcrT); supervised baselines (9Pj4).

Only B: between-block correlation in Figure 4(c) (T4kE); augmentations that ignore factors (Lf3c); β-VAE and FactorVAE baselines (T4kE); the case for discrete channels and EM (T4kE).

Both committees raised limited novelty and the lack of interpretable factors on real data. Both treated these as reasons for a borderline score, not for rejection. Both also doubted the disentanglement, from two sides. A asked what in the method enforces it, and the paper offers only an argument. B showed that the figure does not support it. Neither doubt moved a score.
