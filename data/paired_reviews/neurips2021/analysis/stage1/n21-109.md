**Verdict.** Committee A recommended a poster and Committee B a rejection. Neither committee found a technical error. They weighted the same two facts in opposite directions. First, the framework joins existing parts: R-BCQ "combines" BCQ and REM (L61-63), and λ-generalization applies a multi-objective RL idea (L155). Second, all evidence comes from one application, a coupon simulator and a Taobao Deals A/B test. A's three reviewers did not raise either fact as a weakness. A's chair called the application "well motivated" and "of interest to NeurIPS audience". B drew two reviewers who raised both facts, Reviewer_34tS (5) and Reviewer_gJ2K (4). B's chair took their reasons over a 7 and a 6 and pointed to "a more applied venue". The split comes from reviewer draw and from how each chair valued an applied paper with modest methodological novelty.

**Committee A (poster).** The ratings were 8, 6, 6, and the chair cited them: "All reviewers are inclined to accept". This support is thin. Reviewer_BTic gave the 8 at confidence 3 and wrote "I am not an expert in off-line RL". BTic asked how R-BCQ differs from BCQ and misread Table 3 as showing that AvgCost "decreased significantly". All methods cost 4.05 to 4.08 Yuan against a 4.1-Yuan budget, and L368 calls the costs "similar". Reviewer_r7q2 gave a 6 at confidence 5 after one hour, with a three-sentence main review that restates the abstract. Reviewer_yK9B asked seven clarification questions; the paper already gives δ = 0.1 (L345) and the simulation λ grid (L249). No reviewer judged novelty against BCQ and REM. The chair called the existence proof "natural" and added a missing citation, but accepted on interest in the application. No rating changed after the rebuttal.

**Committee B (reject).** The ratings were 5, 7, 6, 4 (mean 5.5), a 2-2 split. Reviewer_34tS called R-BCQ "only a combination of two popular models", which the paper confirms. 34tS also said no experiment tests λ-generalization. That is wrong: Table 1 compares it with λ-update (3000 against 14000 episodes, 0.44 h against 2.19 h). Reviewer_gJ2K granted that the method removes "a clear computational bottleneck". But gJ2K saw no evidence beyond coupons and proposed "a more applied conference". The authors conceded that wider application "is our future work". Reviewer_1JJe (7) left "the judgement of its technical contributions to other CMDP experts". Reviewer_XKos (6) called the method novel and asked for a CQL baseline, which is absent. No B reviewer replied to the rebuttal. The chair repeated the two lower reviews and did not mention the rebuttal.

**Issues raised by one committee only.** Only B raised:

- incremental novelty, "three tricks" rather than one framework (34tS, chair);
- no evidence beyond coupon allocation, and fit for an applied venue (gJ2K, chair);
- no CQL baseline (XKos), and no architecture for the generative model G (gJ2K);
- use of age and gender features (34tS; L339 confirms);
- unreleased real data and a simulator too simple to matter (34tS).

B also wanted related work in the main text (1JJe, XKos). Only A raised equal discount factors in objective and constraint, the λ grid and its L-fold data growth, whether λ-generalization reaches the λ-update optimum, and the missing Archak et al. (2012) citation. Both asked how R-BCQ and REME relate to BCQ and REM, why results use λ = 0, 0.5 and 1, and whether retention is the right reward. A treated these as questions; B treated them as weaknesses.
