**Committee A (reject).** Reviewer_XvYF and 7k3s called the method a small step from Gouk et al. XvYF also called the self-labeling a minor change to known methods. The paper's own related work cites self-adaptive training (Huang et al., 2020), which also corrects and reweights noisy labels. 7k3s found that the paper stated a spectral-norm constraint while the code used the Frobenius norm. The authors conceded this "typo". 7k3s then found that the proof takes the spectral norm of a Gaussian matrix to be σ. The authors' reply of 2021-09-02 did not answer this point. The camera-ready thanks the program chairs for flagging "an omitted factor in equation (14)". Its bound now carries the layer width H. 7k3s's doubt about a weight-space KL is unfounded; that step is standard PAC-Bayes. Reviewer_R7en's 4 rests on clarity questions, and its last comment defers to the others. XvYF and Reviewer_xra6 never replied. The AC's two sentences restate the consensus reasons and omit the proof error.
