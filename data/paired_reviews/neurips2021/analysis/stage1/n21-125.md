**Verdict.** Both committees saw the same facts and weighted them differently. Every reviewer found the paper clear and its 32-task D4RL total strong (1397 against CQL's 1245). Reviewers on both sides saw that each regularizer is close to prior work and that the value penalty alone trails CQL. Both were pointed to the same ablation as the only case for combining the two. A's reviewers rated the paper 6, 7, 6, 6 at first review, and none moved. B's reviewers started lower and asked for more rigour. The rebuttal lifted Reviewer_4YAR to 6, but B still ended at 5, 5, 6, 6. B's area chair (AC) called the experiments "nicely addressed" but rejected on framing and on the unexplained combination. That AC sized the writing changes as "rather large" and found the novelty pitch "harder to judge without seeing a new revision". A's AC accepted on the same kind of promised text. B's concern is accurate: the camera-ready still rests the combination on the ablation alone.

**Committee A (poster).** No review changed after posting. Reviewer_XtLh (confidence 5) reported that each component trails its prior counterpart: policy constraint alone 299 against BCQ's 1060, value constraint alone 555 against CQL's 1245. The authors answered with framing, not numbers, and XtLh kept 6. Reviewer_T9dA found the closest prior work, Gulcehre et al. **Committee B (reject).** B's reviewers spent 29.5 hours against A's 16. Reviewer_4YAR (12 hours, confidence 5) argued that the value penalty is CQL's with a max over sampled actions and a rectifier. Eq. 6 confirms this and adds a square. The rebuttal added 5 seeds, a rectifier-removal ablation and a behaviour-cloning limit, and 4YAR rose to weak accept. Reviewer_ZoHU asked for "more principled reasoning" on the two regularizers and never replied. Reviewer_dkx5 asked for per-task η and λ. The authors first described the architecture, then gave the values after his last post. Reviewer_meGC (confidence 3) credited the new seeds in a late edit that states no rating change. The AC's two decisive themes come from ZoHU, dkx5's first con and 4YAR. By then 4YAR asked only for "a bit more discourse".

Only B noted that CDC trails on expert data; camera-ready Table 1 confirms this on all six Gym expert and medium-expert tasks.
