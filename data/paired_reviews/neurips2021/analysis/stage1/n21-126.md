**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster for the same reasons. Both saw a narrow but clean theoretical result: the first explicit method with an O(1/k²) rate on the squared gradient norm under negative comonotonicity, and a smaller constant than EAG when ρ = 0. Both saw the same limits. The theory covers only ρ > −1/(2L). No machine-learning problem is shown to satisfy the assumption. The submission has no experiments (checklist 3(a): "We did not run experiments"). The analysis stays close to EAG and EG+. The committees weighed these facts the same way. A reached a poster with no score change. In B, the rebuttal lifted two reviewers from below 6 to 6. The agreement is robust: without A's two 1-hour reviews, A has two 7s from 4- and 5-hour reviews; without ifPf, B has 8, 7 and 6. No reviewer found an error that affects a result.
