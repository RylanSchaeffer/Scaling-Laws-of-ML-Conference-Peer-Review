**Verdict.** Committee A recommended a spotlight and Committee B a reject. The two reviewer pools weighed the same step beyond DH-VQG [19] in opposite ways, and only B checked the evaluation. DH-VQG is the authors' own unpublished double-hints model: four of its seven authors wrote this paper. A's reviewers accepted the paper's framing and asked mostly clarity questions. Three of B's reviewers judged the GAN reward an incremental addition to DH-VQG. The paper confirms B's checkable points. The human study omits DH-VQG and puts DH-GAN level with its own Generator on syntax and semantics. Data augmentation gains are 0.1 to 0.2 points at half the training data. No A reviewer rated above 7, so the spotlight comes from A's area chair (AC), not from the reviews. B's reading fits the paper better. The folder has no submitted PDF, so I checked claims against the camera-ready, which still lacks the promised DH-VQG comparisons.

**Committee A (spotlight).** Ratings 7, 6, 7, 7 (mean 6.75); 7.5 hours per review on average. The reviews praise the idea ("seems very novel", Reviewer_6a7g) and the "comprehensive evaluation" (Reviewer_pdQY). No reviewer compared the margins with DH-VQG or noticed its absence from the human study and Section 3.4. Reviewer_Kags claimed confidence 5 but listed mainly typos. Reviewer_pdQY asked for the visual-hint F1 results, the direct test of the central claim. The authors replied that no ground truth exists, and nobody pressed. No score moved: Kags wrote "I stick to my original score", and two reviews predate the response. The meta-review paraphrases Reviewer_pdQY and Reviewer_6a7g. It repeats pdQY's error that double-hint VQG is "new"; the paper credits that setting to Kai et al. [20]. The AC adds "a novel algorithm design I have never seen" but gives no argument for a spotlight.

**Committee B (reject).** Ratings 5, 4, 5, 5 (mean 4.75); 7.25 hours on average. Reviewer_noBr called the GAN formulation "relatively straightforward". Reviewer_6CYj noted that DH-VQG also learns to point at hints, and the rebuttal concedes that the Generator is "roughly" similar to DH-VQG. Reviewer_tJc4 and Reviewer_W4CH asked whether the generated questions help VQA at all. Three reviews were never edited. Reviewer_tJc4's post-discussion note cites "sparse contributions and low significance". Reviewer_W4CH offered to reconsider after the response but never replied. The AC followed the consensus and named the missing DH-VQG comparison in Section 3.4 and in the zero-shot results. The rebuttal called that comparison impossible because "they did not release it". Reviewer_6CYj erred on one point: Table 3 does isolate the discriminator (22.60 to 23.53 BLEU-4).

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only B checked the size of the gains. Over DH-VQG, Table 1 shows +1.28 BLEU-4 on VQA2.0 and +0.37 on COCO-QA, with no variance reported. Against its own Generator, DH-GAN scores 4.68 vs 4.67 (syntax), 4.65 vs 4.66 (semantics) and 4.00 vs 3.86 (relevance), with undefined spreads near 0.6. Figure 3 shows gains of 0.1 to 0.35 points, except BAN at 30% (+1.4). Only B questioned VQG as a task and the lack of a diversity measure. Only A asked for direct evidence that the GAN selects better visual hints, and A accepted a non-answer. Both committees received the same zero-shot table. A's AC called the rebuttal "a particularly good job". B's AC said the results "again miss comparison to prior work". The committees weighted the same evidence differently.
