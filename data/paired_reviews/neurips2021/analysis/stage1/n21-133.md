**Verdict.** Both committees recommended a spotlight, so there is no split to explain. The agreement comes from the two area chairs, not from matching reviewer evidence. Committee B's spotlight follows its scores: mean 7.0, with two 8s. Committee A's spotlight sits above its scores: 6, 6, 6, 7, with no confidence above 3. Both chairs judged one fact decisive. Gradient descent on a shallow ReLU network is consistent and calibrated on arbitrary noisy distributions. Both committees heard the same weaknesses and treated them as minor. These were the lazy (NTK) regime, a half-page theorem, a univariate early-stopping argument and no estimate of R. One asymmetry did not change the outcome. Only B learned that Theorem 1.1, as submitted, omitted a √(t·R(U∞)) term. B accepted the fix on trust.
