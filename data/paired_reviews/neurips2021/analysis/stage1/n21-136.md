## Verdict

Both committees saw the same paper the same way; the tier split is AC discretion at a mean of 7.0. Each panel gave one 8, two 7s and one 6. Each praised the simple oblivious algorithm and the near-tight bounds, and each noted missing experiments and expectation-only guarantees. Both ACs knew of the concurrent ICML 2021 paper by Makarychev and Shan and called it independent work. Neither meta-review gives a reason for its tier. The difference is what the reviewers knew. In A, the best-informed reviewer, Reviewer_skXW (8, 10 h, read the concurrent papers), wrote that the paper was scooped by work with "very similar results, both in terms of bounds as well as algorithms", and that acceptance was a "policy" question. The camera-ready's own Section 1.3 confirms it: that paper has essentially the same k-medians algorithm and bounds better by log k/log log k. Such a record supports acceptance, not a highlight. In B, no reviewer mentions the concurrent work, so every score treats the paper as the new state of the art. The one stated barrier to a strong accept was withdrawn in discussion, and the AC treated the overlap as a related-work fix. A's poster rests on better-informed reviews.

## Committees

Committee A (8, 7, 6, 7; confidence 4 throughout). Reviewer_skXW rates the paper "in isolation" as a clear accept, but calls explainable clustering "an artificial problem" and says the gains over the concurrent work are polylog factors. Reviewer_tp15 (7, 4 h) asks correct, specific questions: l1 rather than Euclidean k-medians, expectation-only bounds, no experiments, no conclusion. Reviewer_X5Zn (6, 2 h) calls the results "incremental" without argument, although its own summary reports an O(k) to O(log^2 k) gain. Reviewer_Hp5d (7, 2 h) writes an accurate summary but a three-sentence evaluation. Both edits predate the rebuttal, and no reviewer answered it. The two-sentence meta-review answers skXW's concurrency point and asks for a comparison.

Committee B (7, 6, 7, 8). Reviewer_65No (7) is structured and positive, and checked that the prior algorithm is "much more intricate". Reviewer_gBnK (6, confidence 3, 10 h) found a real typo in Eq. (1), which the authors conceded; its main concern, expectation-only bounds, matches tp15's in A. Reviewer_fBhF (7, 1.5 h) would give "a strong accept but for the lack of empirical evaluation". On 2021-08-19 he agreed experiments are not needed, but the 7 stayed. The meta-review cites "sufficient positive feedback and support", raises the Makarychev and Shan overlap itself, sets it aside as independent work, and gives no reason for a spotlight.
