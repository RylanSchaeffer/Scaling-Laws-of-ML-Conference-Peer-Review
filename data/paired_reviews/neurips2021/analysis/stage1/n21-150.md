**Verdict.** Both committees recommended a poster with the same mean rating, 6.5, for the same reasons. Reviewers on both sides saw the first end-to-end generalization guarantee for a deep network on nonlinear low-dimensional data. Its core is a new proof that depth localizes the NTK, which solves the certificate problem that Buchanan et al. left open. Both sides also saw the same weaknesses: NTK regime only, one-dimensional curves only, width L^99 and samples L^10, a depth bound exponential in curvature with unspecified constants, and no training experiments. Each majority called these future work. The committees differed only in spread. B's spread comes from one thin detractor and one misreading. A spotlight was never in play. A's top score is 7. B's two 8s come with "on the fence" (Reviewer_xaXB) and "relies heavily" on Buchanan et al. (Reviewer_qqCj).

**Committee A (poster).** Ratings 7, 6, 7, 6. All four reviews are specific, and all four reviewers answered the rebuttal. Reviewer_2zgs read the depth dependence correctly (lines 233-235) and worried about the NTK regime. Reviewer_skyx and Reviewer_K4pY flagged the L^99 width; Theorem 3.2 confirms it. The authors traced it to the borrowed dynamics theorem, and both kept their scores. K4pY also found that "injectivity radius" clashes with the Riemannian term; the camera-ready renames it. Reviewer_t2Mz found the step-size typo at line 140, the Figure 2 error and a missing citation, all real. Its text still ends "not ready for publication", but on 2021-08-20 it wrote "Increase my score" and now stands at 6. The rebuttal ignored skyx's request for experiments; skyx kept 7. The AC's short meta-review leans on the consensus and calls the concerns minor.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 8, 4, 6, 8. No B reviewer posted in discussion. Reviewer_qqCj (8) wrote a positive, substantive review with questions on SGD and hinge loss. Reviewer_xaXB (8) gave the sharpest counterargument in either committee: sufficiently local classical kernels could learn the same curves. Its "on the fence" text sits oddly with an 8; it edited on 2021-08-31 without comment. Reviewer_haaa (6, confidence 2) said the depth bound falls as the clover number rises. Theorem 3.1 shows the opposite: the base Delta*sqrt(1+kappa^2) is below 1 and the exponent is -C''V. haaa kept 6 after the correction. Reviewer_jk5n (4) is the outlier. Its main claim, that the authors never discuss their sufficient conditions, is wrong: lines 226-240 discuss them. It asks for toy examples, which Figures 2 and 3 give. Its valid points, the huge depth and the line-122 typo, A's reviewers also made without rejecting. jk5n never answered the rebuttal. The AC restated jk5n's concerns, gave no reason to accept, and still followed the majority.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A asked why same-curve pairs enter the injectivity radius and clover number (t2Mz), whether Theorem 3.2 covers kernel drift during training (K4pY), and for the Arora et al. (2019) citation (t2Mz). Only A caught the terminology clash. Only B questioned novelty over Buchanan et al. (jk5n), raised local kernels and depth separation (xaXB), SGD and hinge loss (qqCj), and readability (haaa). None of these changed an outcome. Both committees missed one fact. The authors disclosed, in both forums, that both theorems need an unstated assumption: the curves lie in a spherical cap of radius pi/2. No reviewer responded. The camera-ready adds it to Theorems 3.1 and 3.2.
