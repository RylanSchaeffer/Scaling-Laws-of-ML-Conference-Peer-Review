**Verdict.** The committees weighed the same gap differently. The submission tests SAU only against the four best methods of [18]. It neither cites nor runs NeuralUCB, NeuralTS or hypermodels, and its regret bound covers only multi-armed bandits. Both committees saw the NeuralUCB gap. In B, Reviewer_yDN2 raised it as missing related work, accepted a conceptual and cost argument without any experiment, and raised its score. B's 7/6/7 then became a consensus accept. In A, three reviewers wanted the comparison run. The reviewer discussion made Reviewer_opYp hold at 6 instead of raising. That left a 2-2 split (6/5/5/7). The AC broke it by downweighting Reviewer_R6yL's confidence-2 accept and rested the reject on the missing baselines. A's concern is a correct reading of the paper; the camera-ready still has no NeuralUCB or NeuralTS experiment. B's accept leans on a thin 7 and a meta-review that calls the theory "compelling" without noting its scope.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 6, 5, 5, 7 at confidence 4, 4, 4, 2. Reviewer_bpP1's 5 rests on the missing regret analysis for contextual bandits. The paper confirms it: lines 164-167 bound regret for multi-armed bandits only, and Proposition 4 bounds the residual against MSE, not regret. The authors conceded this. Reviewer_TBN4's 5 rests on missing baselines and no Wheel-bandit test; both hold. TBN4 also found the reversed inequality in Proposition 3. That bound is also undefined, because log(δ/2) is negative; no reviewer noticed. opYp's own questions were answered, but opYp kept 6 after the discussion "made me realise that I had overlooked the important missing baselines". R6yL said the area is outside its expertise and kept 7 while urging the baselines. The meta-review says the authors "did not provide an empirical comparison". That is inaccurate. The authors claimed in prose that SAU beat NeuralTS and NeuralUCB by over 50% and was marginally better on the Wheel bandit. They gave no tables or regret values, and no reviewer credited the claims. The AC set aside bpP1's theory objection as "not deemed crucial".

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 7, 6, 7 at confidence 4, 4, 3. Reviewer_vh32 wrote a careful review. It asked which [18] baseline variants were used and why the regrets exceed those in [18]. The authors conceded some discrepancies; vh32 replied only "My rating remains." Reviewer_yDN2 questioned novelty and NeuralUCB. It also found a missing square in Algorithm 1 and no width parameter in Eq. (7); both hold. The rebuttal gave only conceptual and cost answers, and yDN2 raised its score. The novelty answer says τ² is not a variance estimate. But lines 99-100 introduce τ² as an approximation of the arm's noise variance, and line 164 says its expectation tends to σ². Reviewer_fQ5w's 7 rests on one paragraph that names no weakness, and its rebuttal reply is generic. The meta-review cites consensus. It calls SAU "competitive with Thompson sampling in the limit", which holds only for Bernoulli multi-armed bandits.
