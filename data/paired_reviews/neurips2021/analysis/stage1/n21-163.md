**Verdict.** The committees held different facts, and their area chairs (ACs) used them differently. In A, two careful reviewers showed that cascade connections, one of the paper's two components, already exist as Inline Subnetwork Collaboration (ISC) in Li et al. 2019. The submission does not cite it, and the authors conceded the overlap. A's AC made this the main reason to reject. No B reviewer raised it. B instead had a thin champion, two detractors whose concrete complaints were partly wrong, and a reviewer who rose to 7 after an experiment that A never saw. B's AC read the paper, said it would give a 7, and overrode the two 5s. Neither committee found a fatal flaw.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 4, 4, 4, 6. Reviewer_etSV (24 hours) and Reviewer_ZimZ (confidence 5) cited ISC. The reference list confirms the gap, and the authors wrote "Indeed, ISC also uses direct connections between ICs". Their defence was mainly the setting: ICs on a frozen pre-trained backbone. etSV also correctly found MSDNet missing and Table 1 mixing FLOPs with time (lines 224-226). It wrongly listed Phuong & Lampert as missing; line 96 cites it. Reviewer_5x5Z gave 4 after two hours. Its points were notation, a wish for theory, and a request for other uses. Eq. 1 is indeed inconsistent, but only in notation. Later 5x5Z called the paper "this wonderful piece of work" and kept 4. Reviewer_CbiF's 6 is specific. The AC says CbiF lowered its score after discussion, but the review was never edited and still reads 6. The AC also asked for ImageNet tests, which Table 2 already reports. No A score moved, and A got no new experiments.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 8, 5, 5, 7. Reviewer_6Qh4 gave 8 with confidence 5 after 1.5 hours. The review is generic praise. It calls related work "adequate" and the equations correct. Both statements are wrong: ISC and MSDNet are missing, and the HI_m formula uses an intersection where the text says "any". The camera-ready adds both citations and changes the formula to a union. 6Qh4 never joined the discussion. Reviewer_AQfs (10 hours) said ImageNet and the ablation were missing. Both exist: Table 2, and lines 55-56 point to the ablation in the Appendix. AQfs conceded ImageNet but kept 5 for missing comparisons with non-early-exit methods. Reviewer_Z6co also claimed no ablation. It correctly saw that the geometric ensemble is a weighted logit sum: Eq. 2 reduces to a softmax of a learned weighted sum of IC logits plus a class bias. After the rebuttal Z6co kept 5 and named no remaining concern. Reviewer_WikB moved from "around the borderline" to 7 after a Retinal OCT experiment. Those images are not the huge, time-critical images WikB had asked about. The AC checked the claims, discounted the errors and accepted.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A raised ISC, MSDNet-type baselines, the fairness of the extra IC and ensemble training (etSV), and pruning as a stronger alternative on ImageNet (CbiF). Only A noted that single ICs beat early exiting on Pong in Figure 3 (ZimZ); the figure confirms it. Only B raised model cascades and dynamic routing (AQfs), PABEE as a voting ensemble (Z6co), and time-critical domains (WikB). Only B's AC caught the HI_m error. Both committees doubted the novelty and the narrow baselines. A's AC treated these doubts as decisive; B's AC treated them as fixable.
