**Verdict.** The committees weighted the same facts differently, and only B's reviewers engaged with the rebuttal. Before the rebuttal the panels were level: A rated 7, 5, 5 (mean 5.67) and B 6, 5, 6, 6 (mean 5.75). Both credited the handling of non-unique TD fixed points. Both faulted the Q-learning half for synchronous sampling from a generative model and a one-sentence case for Assumption 3. These criticisms are correct (Algorithm 2; lines 268-269), but they are scope limits, not errors. The authors sent both committees nearly the same rebuttal. Its synchronous-setting paragraph to Vg8w (A) and to ZKyN (B) is word for word the same. In B, qoyS and ZKyN replied and raised 6→7 and 5→6, and the area chair (AC) accepted on "enough support". In A, no reviewer replied. A's AC rejected on Vg8w's point that the two halves are disconnected, which the rebuttal never answered. It added its own view that the TD half alone is not enough for NeurIPS.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 7, 5, 5; no review was edited. Reviewer_wBw2 (7) checked the TD proofs briefly and asked about three appendix steps. The appendix is not in either PDF, so I could not check them. wBw2 declined to judge the Q-learning half. Reviewer_2V8c (5) called the TD half "not that hard" and also declined to judge Q-learning originality. Its points hold: Lemma 2 is the positive-definiteness step, lines 183-184 say the ideas are "inspired by [14]", and lines 65-69 build the Q-learning Lyapunov function on [17]. Its inference is wrong: Theorem 2 still counts generative-model samples, the standard setting of [17, 43]. The meta-review presents Vg8w's disconnection point as what "reviewers believe". It does not mention the rebuttal.

**Committee B (poster).** Final ratings 7, 6, 6, 6. Reviewer_qoyS (7, ten hours) gave the most specific critique. Its claims are correct: Theorem 2 scales as (1−γ)^-5, against (1−γ)^-4 in Li et al. 2021, and has only a decaying step size. qoyS accepted that γ is a span-contraction factor, not a horizon, and raised 6→7. The camera-ready adds the promised constant-step bound and a TD experiment. Reviewer_ZKyN made A's two objections: a standard TD proof and a generative model. It raised 5→6 with a one-line "addressed all my concerns". Reviewer_1neJ (6) listed two generic strengths and three questions, one a correct typo catch (line 81), and never replied. Reviewer_bLoK (6, two hours) restated the abstract, including "both constant and diminishing step sizes", which the submitted Q-learning result lacks. The AC's two sentences cite only the support.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A said the two halves are disconnected, questioned the technique beyond [17], and asked for appendix proof steps. Only B raised the age of the cited applications, the (1−γ)^-5 rate, the missing constant step size, control with linear features, misspecification, and experiments. Both raised the generative model, Assumption 3 and the standard TD analysis. B treated these as limits of a first result. A had two reviewers who could not judge the Q-learning half and none who engaged after the rebuttal; it treated them as reasons to reject.
