## Verdict

Committee B's oral rests on Reviewer_rfa1's thinly argued 9 and an area chair who quoted it. Committee A saw the same paper, peaked at 8, and called clarity its main shortcoming. Both committees saw a complete graphical criterion that extends Zhang et al. (2020) to sequential decisions. Both saw dense notation, idealized experiments and strong assumptions (a known causal graph, one policy per step). No reviewer found an error, and no reviewer checked the proofs. So the committees weighted the same facts differently. rfa1 posted two weeks after the others, at confidence 4, and supported a "top 15%" rating with three generic sentences. rfa1 then took no part in the discussion. Without rfa1, B's ratings average 7.0, exactly A's mean. B's meta-review only "recommend[s] acceptance" and gives no reason for an oral.
