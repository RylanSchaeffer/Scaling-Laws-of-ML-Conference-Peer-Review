**Verdict.** The committees weighed the same weaknesses differently, and the reviewer draw set the weights. Reviewers on both sides saw that L2 feature normalization is standard and that D2D-CE has the form of ContraGAN's 2C loss (Reviewer_HH86 in A; Reviewer_mphj and Reviewer_1hzt in B). B's reviewers accepted this because of the results; mphj wrote "Despite the concerns, the authors showed strong experimental results". In A, HH86 and Reviewer_t8Ys (confidence 5) kept 4 after the rebuttal, and the area chair (AC) made originality decisive. t8Ys's specific objections hold on the submission. The paper never defines "early training collapse". The line 108 argument does not follow from Eq. 4, because a rising logit lowers 1−p. Fig. 2b shows the ACGAN gradient norm rising to about 0.5 and then staying flat; the authors conceded it "does not diverge". The paper's own tables add doubt. On ImageNet, plain ACGAN reaches FID 26.35, near BigGAN's 24.68, and normalization alone worsens it to 36.40 (Tables 2 and 4). B's AC cited "impressive performance". Nobody in B examined the authors' batch-2048 ImageNet run, where ReACGAN trails the same-library BigGAN (FID 8.23 against 7.89). No fatal flaw separates the committees.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 4, 4, 7, 5; mean 5.0. t8Ys (2 hours, but specific) rejects for "logical weaknesses", not novelty, and found the ContraGAN difference "clearly described". HH86 rejects for overlap with ContraGAN and for the name. HH86 read the low top-1 accuracy in Table A9 as a weak auxiliary classifier; the authors and B's Reviewer_KmwA describe it as the accuracy of generated images. New runs (a multi-task discriminator, a debiasing ablation) moved nobody. Reviewer_BEb7's 7 is four generic sentences with no weakness and no discussion. It is not evidence of quality. Reviewer_ojXE mostly paraphrases lines 45-55 of the introduction. ojXE asks what the underlines mean, although lines 238-240 define them, and keeps 5 by deferring to "other reviewers". The AC followed the majority and called the collapse definition easy to fix. The AC named originality "the major issue": the normalization analysis stays in "the narrow angle of the ACGAN model", and D2D-CE is not shown to differ from 2C "in their final form". The AC also said the rebuttal results "need more efforts to be blended" into the paper.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 7, 6, 6, 7; mean 6.5; no rating moved. KmwA engaged most. KmwA's requested TAC-GAN test showed that ReACGAN fails on overlapping class supports; KmwA accepted this as a limitation. Reviewer_JFMx doubted that the gradient is "the root cause". New runs showed that lower loss weights and clipping help less than normalization, and JFMx liked "the added analysis". mphj and 1hzt flagged weak novelty ("an effective combination of AC-GAN and ContraGAN") but recommended acceptance. Neither replied to the rebuttal. The AC wrote that the reviewers "agree" the method is "novel", which overstates both reviews. The AC accepted on novelty and performance.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A (t8Ys) asked for a definition of collapse and a correct gradient argument. t8Ys also wanted multi-task discriminators with R1, a reason for DiffAug over ADA, and SimCLR-style related work. The submission has none of these. Only A disputed the name (HH86) and the size of the gains (HH86, ojXE). With consistency regularization, ReACGAN loses FID on CIFAR10 and Tiny-ImageNet (Table 1). Only B probed overlapping supports, other backbones, focal-loss similarity and the missing MHGAN row (KmwA, JFMx, mphj). B's rebuttal showed that normalization also repairs TACGAN (Tiny-ImageNet FID 67.86 to 29.07). That is evidence against A's "narrow angle" objection, but A never saw it.
