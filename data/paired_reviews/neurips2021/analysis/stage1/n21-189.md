**Verdict.** Both committees found the same weaknesses, and the submission confirms each one. FREDO needs ground-truth depth, masks and cameras at test time and ground-truth 3D correspondences in training. It is tested on one synthetic dataset. Its baselines got RGB only. It cites no RGB-D non-rigid fusion work. The split comes from who reviewed and how they weighed these facts. Three A reviewers raised DynamicFusion unprompted, all at confidence 4, and A judged absolute quality. B judged the method against learned baselines and valued a first step on a hard task. Both forums got nearly the same rebuttal: 3DPW results on humans and a qualitative SurfelWarp comparison. It moved Reviewer_FhQ8 in B to 7. It moved nobody in A. Each AC followed its reviewers. A's objections were accurate for the paper as submitted. The camera-ready adds what A asked for. Under its revised protocol the margin over OFlow is 5.9 mIoU, not 12.3.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 4, 4, 3, 4. All four answered the rebuttal and held. Their checkable claims hold. The title says "from Monocular Videos" for an RGB-D method (Reviewer_Jqx8). Lines 3-5 call the setting "more realistic" than prior work, yet references [1]-[53] contain no fusion method. In Fig. 5 the motorbike, truck and airplane, shown as successes, are coarse or incomplete despite ground-truth depth (Reviewer_SY53). The authors conceded that nothing makes the output match the input depth. Table 1 gives 50.5 IoU for persons and 20.4 for airplanes, and no unseen class is tested (Reviewer_7c6w). SY53's "ground-truth flow" reading of line 347 is what the text says; the text is wrong. Two points are weak. Jqx8 compared FREDO's full shapes with DynamicFusion's observed surfaces, and called NDG SMPL-based. 7c6w dismissed 3DPW as humans only. No A reviewer and not the AC mentioned the SurfelWarp results, although Reviewer_nToe and SY53 replied after they were posted. The AC made the missing fusion comparison decisive. In the camera-ready SurfelWarp scores 1.03 mIoU, so that gap was about positioning, not results.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 7, 8, 7, 6. Reviewer_Gayd's 8 calls the related work satisfactory and the results strong. It never mentions the ground-truth inputs or the RGB-only baselines, and it did not reply. Reviewer_tafs wrote a careful review at confidence 3, caught the oracle contradiction, and kept 7. Reviewer_Lqt3 (3 hours, confidence 3) raised 5 to 6 but still called the work synthetic-only, ignoring 3DPW. Reviewer_FhQ8 raised the fusion and baseline-fairness gaps that A raised. The 3DPW figures and SurfelWarp results moved it to 7. B's praise of large margins rests on baselines that got no depth. The AC summarised scores. Its account is loose: of the "positive" reviewers only FhQ8 rose, and Lqt3 did raise its score.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A checked the output against the input view, asked for unseen categories, flagged the "Monocular" title and the claims at lines 3 and 79, and asked for Nerfies, NSFF and classical registration baselines. Only B asked about the fixed Z_far offset, window size, positional encoding, run time, occlusion levels and tracked masks. Both saw the ground-truth inputs, the synthetic-only data, the RGB-only baselines, the missing fusion work and the OFlow-plus-PIFu novelty. A treated them as reasons to reject. B treated them as fixes for the final version.
