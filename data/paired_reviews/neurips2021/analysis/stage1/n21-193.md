**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster because they read the paper the same way. Every reviewer accepted the core result: a discrete-time version of NEIS whose estimator of Z is unbiased for any step size (Theorem 1, l.249-254), and a new NEO-MCMC sampler with ergodicity guarantees. Both committees also named the same two weaknesses. The presentation is dense, especially Section 2. The experiments are narrow: the NEIS comparison is only in the appendix, and recent samplers (SNF, Orbital MCMC, Contour SGLD) are absent. These weaknesses held seven of eight final ratings at 6 or 7. B's higher mean (7.0 against 6.5) comes from one rating. Reviewer_n3jg gave a 9 with a 100-word review that calls the experimental comparison "the major drawback". That 9 is not evidence of quality, and B's AC did not act on it; without it, B's mean is 6.33. The errors that reviewers found, and that the paper confirms, are notation, not flaws. The authors posted the same new experiments in both forums, so both ACs saw the same rebuttal evidence and asked for the same revisions.

Committee B (7, 6, 9, 6). No score change is recorded.
