**Verdict.** The two committees saw the same weaknesses and scored them on opposite sides of the threshold. Both found a gap of up to K^γ between upper and lower bounds for γ<2, which lines 105-106 of the submission concede. Both called the tools standard: Laplace noise, a plug-in and a threshold, with no polynomial approximation. Both asked for motivation, clearer rates, the central-DP paper INSPECTRE and support for the entropy claims. Before the rebuttal the means were close (A 5.75, B 5.6). But three of A's four reviewers gave 6 or 7, and three of B's five gave 5. The rebuttal, largely the same text in both forums, moved only A. Reviewer_AFYC rose from 4 to 5, and A's AC credited "new results". AFYC misread them as closing the gap for γ∈(1,2) when √(nα²) ≲ K. There the new lower bound still lies a factor (α²n)^(γ−1) below the upper bound, and the camera-ready says so. In B only Reviewer_ztVm replied, in one line with no reason. B's AC rejected in three sentences on writing and the bound gap. A's AC treated the writing as a camera-ready fix and did not hold the gap against the paper. Reviewer calibration and rebuttal engagement decided the split, not a fact one committee missed.

**Committee A (poster).** Ratings 5, 7, 6, 6 at confidence 4, 4, 4, 3. Reviewer_VFZT's 7 is careful: it caught that Theorem 5.1 takes the supremum over P(2) while claiming any K. Its claim that the interactive estimator is optimal at K ≍ nα² for γ∈(0,2) is wrong; that estimator exists only for γ>1, and there it misses the lower bound. Reviewer_bSYV and Reviewer_j1d8 gave 6 while listing the weaknesses B held against the paper: simple estimators, the gap, thin motivation, hard-to-read rates. AFYC's objection that Theorem 5.1 lacks K-dependence is wrong: for γ≥2 the minimax rate is K-free and the bound is tight (lines 271-272). After the rebuttal AFYC still "tend[s] to recommend rejection" but is "fine to see it accepted too". The AC added its own expertise. It corrected the authors' claim that full interaction turns local DP into central DP, and it demanded a clear interaction model for every bound. The camera-ready does both.

**Committee B (reject).** Ratings 5, 5, 5, 7, 6 at confidence 5, 4, 5, 4, 4. Reviewer_H2n9's 5 rests mainly on venue fit: no ICML or NeurIPS paper treats these functionals. Its own committee refutes this. Reviewer_V6UM and Reviewer_zYFp cite INSPECTRE (ICML 2018) and NeurIPS 2019 papers on the problem. H2n9 also misstates the main result, at confidence 5. It calls the interactive gain "much more better" than the non-interactive minimax rate. The paper claims a log factor and leaves the non-interactive rate open (lines 100-101, 288-291). The 5s of ztVm and V6UM are accurate: unclosed bounds, plug-in variants, missing references, no experiments. Reviewer_zEEc's 7 is accurate but brief. It says "appropriate references are provided" although INSPECTRE is missing, and zEEc did not return after the rebuttal. The AC did not mention the rebuttal.

Only A caught the P(2) typo and called the lower-bound proofs routine uses of [8] and [4]. None of A's extra issues counted against acceptance; A's AC turned the interaction-model point into a camera-ready condition.
