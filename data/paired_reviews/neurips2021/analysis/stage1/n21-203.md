**Verdict.** The two committees saw the same paper: a clear, well-written pooling layer with tidy theory and small, uneven gains. They weighted those gains differently, and the reviewer draw set the weights. In A, both confidence-4 reviewers judged the practical value too low, and the submission confirms their checkable points. On ModelNet40 with the ISAB backbone, PSWE ties PMA at 87.6% (Table 1), and the authors' own timings give PSWE twice PMA's inference time (Reviewer_wTMH). PSWE is best in none of the four image settings, and FSPool beats it in three (Table 3). On graphs, a baseline wins 5 of 15 rows and ties one, even with PSWE's best of six L values per row (Table 2; Reviewer_ABo1). A's only champion, Reviewer_qqRn (8), had "very little to add" and later shared the empirical doubts. A's AC added an objection that no reviewer raised and the authors could not answer: PSWE is "just a variant" of Mialon et al.'s OT embedding. The paper gives that work two sentences (lines 94-97) and no comparison. Three of B's four reviewers also named the weak gains ("only equally / slightly better", "very close to GAP"). They rated novelty higher, but none weighed it against Mialon et al. or Kolouri et al. [16], and none noted that PSWE wins no image setting. No fatal flaw separates the committees. A's reading of the experiments is the more accurate one.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 4, 8, 5; mean 5.67. wTMH (8 hours) asked for a random-slicer baseline and a reason for the trainable parts, because Eqs. 7-12 hold for any fixed slices and reference. Both points hold. wTMH's claim that Rabin et al. is missing is wrong: it is reference [38]. The rebuttal's runtime table backfired. ABo1 raised the rating "slightly", to 5, but still wanted local pooling, stronger backbones and larger image benchmarks. The authors' "legal" reason for skipping ImageNet drew objections from both ABo1 and qqRn, since ModelNet40, Places and IMDB carry similar terms. The AC leaned on ABo1 ("comparison with SOTA methods", "local pooling") and added the Mialon point.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 7, 5, 7, 6; mean 6.25. Reviewer_xtA8 called the method "really novel" and promised a 6, but never changed the form. The promise followed a Places-Extra69 rerun that moved every method by roughly 10 to 17 points. In that rerun PSWE is best in neither row (35.2 against FSPool's 35.4; 58.30 against GAP's 58.63). xtA8 also called f_i^θ undefined, but Eq. 7 defines it. Reviewer_vGLg's 7 restates the method and cites no result. Reviewer_iGeu read most closely: the experiments use a weighted sum to L dimensions, stated only in the supplement. Eq. 12's exact-embedding identity fails for that vector, but iGeu treated the point as one of clarity. Reviewer_SDvh kept 6 although the experiments "should meet higher standards". The AC followed a positive discussion and caught that SW comes from [38].

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A judged absolute accuracy. Table 1 tops out at 87.6%, about the level ABo1 cites for PointNet without transforms. Only A raised the cost against PMA, the ImageNet excuse, local pooling and closeness to Mialon et al. B's authors told SDvh they "could not afford to run OTKE", which supports A's AC. Only B found the weighted-sum output and the drop at L = 1024 (IMDB-B with GIN, 74.6 to 68.8). Only B asked for max pooling, which Table 1 also lacks although PointNet uses it, and for controlled tests of OT against MMD. Both asked for ablations of the trainable slicers and reference. Neither saw numbers.
