**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster because they saw the same paper and weighed it the same way. The paper gives simple testers for a new, narrow problem. It has presentation gaps but no error in a result. All seven final ratings are 6 or 7 (A: 7, 6, 7; B: 7, 7, 6, 6). So neither committee had a champion for a higher tier or a detractor for rejection. In each committee, one reviewer doubted that the problem matters and settled at 6. The technical points overlap. The stated sample complexity, O(m^-1/2), falls below the two samples that the paper says are necessary. The paper gives no method to compute φ_ε, no explicit ε-dependence and no lower bounds. The authors answered the φ_ε question with the same paragraph in both forums. The errors that the paper confirms are in side remarks, and none breaks a result. B wrote about twice as many review words (1,734 against 827) and found more small faults. Both ACs wrote short meta-reviews that follow the scores.

**Committees**

Committee A (7, 6, 7). Reviewer_nEjk's 7 is thin. It says "I read all the proofs" but names no step, and it recommends acceptance "mainly" because the setting is interesting. Reviewer_ExtA raised only significance. The rebuttal gave a fair-lending use case and Mukherjee (2016), and ExtA's update calls this "much-needed motivation" (final 6). Reviewer_efTh wrote the only technical review, and its checkable points hold. The introduction (l.62-63) says that TRUNC beats bucketing for small m. But in Figure 3, TRUNC is below BUNIF over most of the m = 10 panel. The bounds drop the constant, although l.163-165 say that one sample cannot be enough. The private experiments stop at m = 1000. After the rebuttal, efTh raised the score to 7. The AC's four sentences add only efTh's remark on lower bounds.

Committee B (7, 7, 6, 6). Reviewer_G2iJ found that l.202 gives a Laplace variance four times too large. The authors conceded. Reviewer_Czjv (one hour) correctly flagged the sub-one sample complexity. But it misstated the 2SAMP condition: the paper needs φ_ε ≤ 1 − Ω(1/m), not φ "large enough". Reviewer_r9MB wrote the most detailed review: two missing citations, no method for φ_ε, unclear ε-dependence and unknown optimality. The authors admitted that φ_ε has no closed form in ε and m. r9MB did not reply and stayed at 6. Reviewer_C58N posted on 2 August, after the others, and reversed the test. It says that the statistic is large under the null, but Algorithm 2 rejects when Y is large. Its question whether 2SAMP is redundant is fair, because UNIF's k also falls to 2 for large m. The AC called the concerns "low-level" and did not mention C58N's doubt about significance.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised the TRUNC claim against Figure 3, the m ≤ 1000 limit of the private experiments, and the missing applications. Only B raised these issues:

- the Laplace variance error and the overloaded ε and δ (G2iJ);
- the missing Valiant and Valiant (2017) and Batu and Canonne (2017) citations (r9MB);
- why the test uses only disjoint consecutive pairs, and the unclear text after Theorem 5 (r9MB);
- the redundancy of 2SAMP (C58N);
- typos (Czjv).

The checkable items hold; the others are requests for clarity. A's issues came from one careful reviewer, efTh. B's came from four reviewers who each found different small faults. None of these issues changed a decision, because none touches a theorem or proof.
