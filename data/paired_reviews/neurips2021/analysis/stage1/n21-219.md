**Verdict**

Committee A's spotlight is the anomaly; B's poster follows its record. A rated the paper 7, 6 and 7 (mean 6.67). No A reviewer rated above 7 or argued for a higher tier. A's meta-review says the paper "pushes forward the research" and recommends that it "be accepted". It gives no reason for a spotlight, so the tier rests on the AC's own view. B rated the paper 7, 4, 8 and 6 (mean 6.25). B's supporters were not cooler than A's reviewers: without Reviewer_c6Mx, B's mean is 7.0. The difference is that B drew confident critics of novelty and A drew none. Both committees saw the same paper: many new rates in separate settings, built from known tools. A weighted the breadth. B weighted the simplicity of the parts and the missing common story.

**Committees**

Committee A (7, 6, 7; spotlight). Reviewer_vTqw (6 h, confidence 4) wrote the only technical review. It found that the first term of the Theorem 10 bound decreases with d, and the submitted abstract confirms this. The authors refined the bound, and the camera-ready uses the refined form. vTqw wrote "My concerns are well addressed" and edited its review to a final 7. Reviewer_KmGd (2 h, confidence 2) wrote three lines; its one weakness was "too many results". Reviewer_Dzyq did not track hours and made no specific point. Neither replied to the rebuttal. Nobody in A questioned novelty. The weaker ℓ2 rate against [ZCH+20] appeared only in the authors' reply to vTqw. The PDF is the camera-ready main text, so vTqw's appendix points stay unchecked.

Committee B (7, 4, 8, 6; poster). Reviewer_c6Mx (4, confidence 4) argued incrementality, and the paper confirms two of its points. The ℓ2 GLL result is 1-D Moreau smoothing plugged into the Phased-SGD of [FKT20]. The paper calls this "the same simple idea", and the authors conceded. The ℓ2 non-convex rate is "slightly worse than the state of the art" [ZCH+20] (p.3). One point is wrong: the weakly convex n^{-1/4} rate matches the best non-private rate, so its gap to the smooth case shows nothing. Reviewer_TpjV (6, confidence 4, 10 h) added very large batches, no experiments and doubts about the novelty of the private Frank-Wolfe. Reviewer_MuL7's 8 is substantive. It caught the unstated projection oracle in Theorem 16 and drew a lower-bound insight from the GLL result. Reviewer_Brcd's 7 (confidence 2) is thin. No B reviewer replied in public, and c6Mx's 4 stands. The AC noted "a lot of discussion" and asked for one story from "diverse-seeming results".

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised the d-decreasing term in Theorem 10, the moments accountant, gaps in the appendix proofs, and the value of the ℓp geometry. All came from vTqw, all were clarifications, and the authors answered all of them.

Only B raised the incrementality of the GLL result, the novelty of the private Frank-Wolfe, the missing privacy amplification, large batches, missing experiments, missing lower bounds, and the implicit projection oracle. These issues bear on significance, and they explain why B stopped at poster.

Two issues came up in both committees. A's KmGd and Dzyq flagged the fragmented presentation, but A's AC ignored it; B's AC made it the main message. On [ZCH+20], A heard the comparison only from the authors, while B's TpjV and c6Mx counted it as a weakness.
