**Verdict.** The committees weighted the same facts differently, and both ACs turned a moderate rating gap (mean 6.5 against 5.25) into a spotlight-versus-reject split. Both committees saw a clear, timely CKA study of ViT against two BiT ResNets. It explains its findings (global attention, skip connections, the Figure 8 intervention) but gives no design guidance, and it covers one CNN family. Committee A's most careful reviewer, Reviewer_sCgD, raised the one-family and depth points and judged neither "severe enough" to reject. Committee B applied a test that A did not: an analysis paper must help design or improve architectures. All four B reviewers said the paper fails that test. B's lowest score (Reviewer_16Eb, 4, two hours) rests partly on misreadings. A's AC made 7/6/7/6 a spotlight in three sentences that cite only timeliness and unanimity. B's AC broke a two-two split toward reject. That AC wrote that "the reviewers all said" the study was not thorough on other CNNs. Only 16Eb and fR12 said so, and fR12 and MKzA called the analysis "thorough" and "comprehensive". No reviewer on either side found an error in a result. Claims were checked against b/paper_submitted.pdf; a/ holds only the camera-ready.

**Committee A (spotlight).** Ratings 7, 6, 7, 6; no review was edited. Reviewer_qGAU (7, confidence 5) wrote a short positive review with three fair extension requests: hybrid models, a convolutional stem, and the skip-removal test on ResNet. Reviewer_qxEj's 7 is thin praise, with no weaknesses and no reply after the rebuttal. Reviewer_SJG2 posed nine concrete questions. One premise is wrong: the BiT ResNets use group norm, not batch norm. The authors answered every point and added CKA heatmaps with channel-normalized ResNet features. SJG2 stayed at 6 and posted nothing more. Reviewer_sCgD (7 hours) gave the sharpest critique, and two of its points are correct. The "representative set of CNN" models (L65-66) are ResNets only. The claim that early local attention matters for performance (L129-133) is correlational and untested. sCgD kept 6 and argued against rejection.

**Committee B (reject).** Ratings 4, 6, 6, 5. Reviewer_16Eb says the paper does not show "why this kind of representation emerges". Sections 5-6 address exactly this, and Figure 8 tests it by removing skip connections. 16Eb also treats the 10-shot linear probes as the paper's classification evaluation; they are an analysis tool. 16Eb's valid point, one CNN family, is the point sCgD judged non-fatal. Reviewer_fR12 (5) called the main claims "not extremely surprising" and wanted design guidance and detection experiments. The paper already answers two of fR12's six weaknesses: per-head distances (Figure 3) and the ERF definition (Figure 6 caption). Reviewer_NaL7 (6, confidence 5) wrote a two-sentence main review. NaL7 correctly noted that T2T-ViT, CaiT and DeepViT are not cited, but named the wrong T2T-ViT figure. Reviewer_MKzA (6) misread the conclusion's sentence on Section H. MKzA wrote "I rate the score to 6" and edited the review on 2021-08-11; the earlier score is not recoverable. 16Eb and fR12 kept their scores after the rebuttal.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only B demanded practical guidance for architecture design. In A, Reviewer_qxEj instead praised the work because it "might inspire new architectures". Only B (NaL7) flagged missing concurrent work with overlapping observations. Only A probed the method itself: the normalization confound (answered with new heatmaps), the correlational locality claim, the CKA-versus-probe tension, the Figure 5 choice of block, and JFT-300M reproducibility. A's method points are more specific and better grounded than B's, and A still accepted. B's decisive points are a standard for analysis papers and the one-family limit, which both committees saw.
