**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster for the same reasons. The paper proves that Gaussian-padded affine-coupling flows can approximate log-concave targets with a well-conditioned Jacobian. Reviewers in both committees called the proof technique novel, and nobody found an error beyond typos. Most reviewers named the same limits: log-concave targets only, and no experiments. No final rating is below 6 or above 7, so neither committee had a champion or a detractor. The committees weighted the same facts differently, and the difference stayed inside the poster band. Committee A gave three 6s (mean 6.0): a useful technique, but a narrow result. Committee B gave six 7s and one 6 (mean 6.86) for the same reading. Reviewer_PyGo, for example, calls the result unsurprising and the technique the contribution, and gives 7. B's thin reviews do not cause the gap: B's five substantive reviewers all gave 7. Neither committee weighed two points that the paper confirms. First, Theorem 1 (l.107) needs I ⪯ −∇² ln p ⪯ κI, a strongly log-concave and smooth target. That is narrower than "any log-concave distribution" in the abstract. Second, the rebuttal gave a depth bound of polylog(ε)/ε^O(κ), and nobody commented on its size.

**Committees**

Committee A (6, 6, 6). Reviewer_enbB (confidence 4) wrote A's most expert review. It first gave a borderline reject because log-concavity "limits the scope". It also found a real error: as printed, eq (12) updates x with v_n, so it is not the two-block composition that l.306 claims. The authors called it a typo and offered a narrower title, and enbB rose to 6. The camera-ready title adds "For Log-concave Distributions". Reviewer_ts1e called the paper "original, high quality, clearly written, and significant" but gave 6. The only limitation it names is the log-concave scope. Reviewer_Hnha (confidence 2) said the proof "lies outside of my expertise", so its 6 carries little information. The meta-review follows enbB's post-rebuttal view: the scope "remains limited", but the work is a foundation for future theory.

Committee B (7, 7, 7, 7, 6, 7, 7). Reviewer_NQpX, Reviewer_PyGo and Reviewer_rkxh wrote careful reviews, named the same log-concavity limit, and gave 7. rkxh noticed that Theorem 1 bounds the conditioning of the whole network only. The authors conceded that they have no per-layer guarantee, and nobody returned to the point. Reviewer_yz9H rose to 7 after the replies on log-concavity and on other flow families. Two reviews carry little weight. Reviewer_Z2tC (confidence 2) wrote generic praise with no question. Reviewer_5zdr wrote "I have checked the technique parts" but named no step. The meta-review lists strengths only.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised these issues:

- the title overclaims "universal approximation" (enbB);
- the eq (12) error and the missing v_0 in Lemma 1 (enbB; the paper confirms both);
- prior work: Huang et al. already noted the singularity, Zhang et al. is a neural-ODE result, and Teshima et al. is listed but never cited (enbB, ts1e);
- polynomial rather than neural-network non-linearities (ts1e, enbB).

Only B raised these issues:

- no conditioning guarantee for single layers (rkxh; conceded);
- the optimal-transport map as an alternative, and extension to continuous and residual flows (NQpX, yz9H);
- missing work on the origin of flows and on ill-conditioned flows (PyGo);
- a sign error in eq (1) and a missing γ at l.250 (rkxh; both confirmed).

Every error found on either side was a typo or an attribution point. None changed a decision.
