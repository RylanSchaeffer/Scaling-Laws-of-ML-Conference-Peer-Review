**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster for the same reason. All eight reviewers valued the dataset and task, and most judged the curricular method a modest add-on. Each committee had one detractor, and the rebuttal moved both up. In A, Reviewer_33q7 began on the reject side because Efrat et al. had a similar dataset and T5 baseline. The submission calls that work concurrent three times (l.62, l.83, l.317). 33q7 admitted the oversight and rose to 6. In B, Reviewer_T9VZ (5) found a real gap: a character-level task, a subword-tokenized model and no character-level baseline. Tables 2 to 5 confirm the gap. B's AC agreed and deferred it to "the next version". ByT5, the model T9VZ names, appeared only in late May 2021, the month of submission. The committees read the tokenization fact in opposite ways. A's Reviewer_4hbV took it as a sign that the task needs new architectures, and gave 8. T9VZ took it as a reason to doubt the task's difficulty, and gave 5. B's one 8 is weak evidence: 55% of Reviewer_zZBA's words are verbatim spans of the submission. No reviewer argued for more than a poster. Neither committee noticed that the claimed gain over Efrat's best model holds only on the disjoint splits (Table 5, naive split: 52.1 against 53.2), or that every result is a single run (checklist 3(c)).

**Committees**

Committee A (6, 8, 8, 7). Reviewer_4hbV wrote the most detailed review (4 hours). It asked for the data itself, not a scraper; checklist 4(d) confirms that only code was released. It also questioned top-1 metrics. The authors committed to release the data and explained that cryptic answers are unique, and 4hbV rose to 8. Reviewer_2QZg (8) asked for a human evaluation and doubted that a niche puzzle tests human-like thinking. Reviewer_inbY (7, 1 hour, confidence 3) wrote a short, accurate review from a stated prior: "I generally favor accepting new resource papers". After 33q7 withdrew its objection, no argument for rejection remained. The meta-review weighs only the Efrat question and echoes inbY's "carefully constructed splits".

Committee B (7, 5, 8, 7). Reviewer_oTbN (6 hours) found the related work thin and the motivation weak. Section 2 (l.75-85) cites no curriculum or pre-finetuning work. oTbN rose from 6 to 7 on promises to fix both. T9VZ spent 1 hour at confidence 5. It correctly saw that the paper calls T5 a "prefix-LM" (l.65, l.154), a writing error. It wrongly said that the tokenization limitation "is not discussed": Sections 6.1 and 6.3 test length and descrambling, although the text never calls this a limitation. T9VZ rose to 5 only on condition that the rebuttal's arguments enter the paper. Reviewer_toeW (7) found the method close to Muppet-style pre-finetuning and caught the "(7)" typo for PETAL. zZBA's review restates the paper, down to the citation "[6]" and the checklist's "our approach". Its one original point is the missing human comparison. The AC followed the majority.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised these issues:

- novelty against Efrat et al. as a reason to reject (33q7, withdrawn);
- the unreleased dataset (4hbV), released in the camera-ready;
- top-1 metrics and answer uniqueness (4hbV);
- gains beyond anagram clues (33q7) and the niche-task objection (2QZg).

Only B raised these issues:

- the missing character-level baseline (T9VZ, oTbN, AC);
- the prefix-LM error and the "curricular" misnomer (T9VZ);
- thin related work (oTbN, toeW), expanded in the camera-ready;
- missing limitations and real-world motivation (toeW, oTbN).

None of these issues changed a decision.
