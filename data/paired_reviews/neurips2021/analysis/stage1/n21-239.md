**Verdict.** The committees weighed the same central fact differently, and the reviewer draw set the scores. Both saw that Assumption A5 confines the minibatch speedup to well-structured, near-smooth problems and excludes SGD on non-smooth losses. Committee A's reviewers asked for narrower framing and stayed at 6 or above. Committee B's two engaged reviewers also ended at 6. B's mean sat at 4.75 because of two weak low reviews. B's area chair (AC) then rejected on the claim that A5 "is essentially smoothness", which is false for the prox-linear model. One B-only fact has real weight. Reviewer_Kosw showed that Theorem 3.2 nearly duplicates an uncited lemma of Wang, Wang and Srebro (2017), and the authors conceded it. A never saw it. No fatal flaw separates the committees.

**Committee A (poster).** Ratings 6, 6, 8; mean 6.67. Reviewer_5Yxy raised to 6 after the rebuttal but still found the work "limited to special classes of problems". Reviewer_Cw8j kept 6, judged the problems "quite close to those that are smooth", and asked for rephrasing. The authors promised a retitle to "weakly convex" and the removal of A6 and A7. Reviewer_h9LY gave 8 at confidence 3, whose label says the math was "not carefully checked". The 8 rests on clear writing and on a stability analysis "novel to me", which Wang et al. (2017) undercuts. It never weighs the A5 restriction. The 8 did not decide the outcome, because the two 6s already clear the threshold. The AC's meta-review says "The reviewers assessed the paper favorably" and asks for proofreading. It does not mention the restriction.

**Committee B (reject).** Ratings 3, 6, 4, 6; mean 4.75. Reviewer_cuoE spent one hour. It asks how the method differs from "any proximal and regularized gradient step", which ignores that the contribution is a rate. Its "See Section ??" complaint quotes the template's instruction block (lines 379-389), not the authors' answer. After the rebuttal it cited unnamed "important flaws" and did not reply when asked. Reviewer_37oF (4, confidence 5, three hours) says A5 "is never used in the literature" and that the result needs A6 and A7. Remark 2 derives A5 from the standard prox-linear setting, and Remark 3 makes A6 and A7 optional. 37oF never replied. Kosw rose from 5 to 6 for a new boundedness result posted only in B, but still called the minibatch part "too incremental". The AC's smoothness claim holds only for SGD; SPL satisfies A5 on the paper's non-smooth phase retrieval problem. The AC also called the paper unclear to "most of the reviewers", but Kosw and 37oF praised its clarity.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only B found the Wang et al. (2017) precedent (Kosw). Only B noted that momentum gives no rate gain (Kosw, Reviewer_kmZz) and that the speedup stops at large batch sizes (kmZz). Only B asked for error bars and more baselines (Kosw, cuoE). Only B's AC found Theorem 3.4 hard to parse; the authors' promise in B to drop A6 and A7 already exposes the batch-size term. Only A asked about the cost of the minibatch subproblems, especially exact SPP steps (5Yxy, Cw8j). Only A asked for comparisons with Zhang and Xiao (2020) and with Chadha, Cheng and Duchi (2021). Only A questioned the fixed stepsize in Theorem 3.4 and the missing SPP curve in Figure 1 (h9LY).
