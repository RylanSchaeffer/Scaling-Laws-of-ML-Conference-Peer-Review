**Verdict.** Both committees recommended a poster for the same reason. The paper extends the strict benefit of invariance in Elesedy and Zaidi [8] from linear models to kernel ridge regression. Both accepted pure theory without experiments. B's higher mean (7.0 against 6.0) comes mainly from who reviewed and which version they read. The authors put a longer, self-contained version in the supplement. Reviewers whose comments point to that version gave 6 to 8. The three whose comments cite only the 8-page main paper all gave 5, and A had two of them. Each committee also had one weak rating: A's 5 from a self-declared non-expert at confidence 2 (Reviewer_ZLX8), and B's 9 from a four-sentence review (Reviewer_6ik1). Without them, both means are 6.33. Only A tested the main proof. Reviewer_LcrW said that the eigenvalue step at line 241 can fail, and the authors defended it with a wrong argument. LcrW is right: the step holds when (K+ρI)⁻¹ and J⊥ commute, but fails in general. The bias term of Theorem 5 is also false. With a constant target, a Gaussian kernel (width 1), the reflection group on [−1, 1], no noise, n = 20 and ρ = 0.01, E[Δ] is about 3×10⁻⁵ against a claimed 2×10⁻³. The paper's own linear example (camera-ready Theorem 7) fails too. Only the noise term survives. Neither area chair (AC) ruled on this, and the camera-ready keeps the false term.

**Committee A (poster).** Ratings 6, 5, 5, 8. Reviewer_j3tj (6) read the supplement, asked for clarity fixes and examples, and kept 6. Reviewer_LcrW (5, 3 hours) read only the main paper and listed proof gaps. The authors conceded that Theorem 5 needs a bounded target (lines 244, 250) but defended line 241. LcrW edited the review afterwards; no score change is visible. ZLX8 declined to judge the technical quality and kept 5, citing LcrW and the missing experiments. Reviewer_Bqwx's 8 praises "sound maths" and calls the page limit "largely overstepped", which is true only of the supplement. The AC copied j3tj's summary nearly word for word, conceded problems in "presentation and technical quality", and left them to the final version.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 7, 5, 9, 7. Reviewer_xasM (7, 20 hours) read the supplement, "checked the proofs as much as I can", and valued the extension although the main ideas came from [8]. Reviewer_Tnf7 (7, 12 hours) found an unjustified use of Fubini's theorem at line 247. The authors added a bounded target, and Tnf7 noted that a square-integrable target suffices. Tnf7 had offered to raise its score and edited the review on 2021-08-24. Reviewer_uJHC (5) said that real invariances are local and that the paper gives no example; it did not reply. Reviewer_6ik1's 9 is generic praise after 3 hours, with no reply. The AC followed the scores, noting that the "practical meaning is not very clear".

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A questioned the line-241 inequality. Only A complained of haste, notation and differences between the main paper and the supplement (LcrW), and asked for a formal treatment of the kernel condition, Eq. (1) (j3tj). Only B raised local and approximate invariance (uJHC) and the cost of exact orbit averaging (Tnf7). Only B noted the clash with the "effective dimension" of Caponnetto and De Vito, which the paper does not cite (xasM). Both raised the missing experiments, the dependence on [8], the integrability of the target, and the reversed sign of Δ at line 225. LcrW took the integrability gap as evidence of haste; Tnf7 called it minor. That is the same fact weighted differently.
