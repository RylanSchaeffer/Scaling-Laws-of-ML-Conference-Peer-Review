**Verdict.** The committees saw the same paper, the same main weakness and the same rebuttal. They split on whether rebuttal-period experiments count. Six of the eight reviewers called the evaluation thin (all of A; Strq and 8KpE in B), and the submission confirms it: three synthetic problems from Lam & Willcox [1], two myopic baselines, and rollout curves copied from [1] (lines 269-293). Both forums then received a portfolio experiment (2021-08-10/11), and later robot pushing plus four new baselines (2021-09-01/02). In B, the portfolio result lifted Reviewer_HPhn from 6 to 7 and Reviewer_Strq up to 7, and Reviewer_uDRU rose to 6. In A, only Reviewer_wxwg moved, to 5, citing clarifications. A's two 6s never edited, so A had no champion. A's area chair (AC) listed real benchmarks and more baselines as required, and did not mention the ones the rebuttal supplied. B's AC accepted on the rebuttal example. Before the rebuttal no rating in either panel was above 6. No reviewer found a technical error, and the camera-ready contains the new problems and baselines.

**Committee A (reject).** Ratings 6, 5, 4, 6, after 4 to 6 hours each. Reviewer_vkHd (4) is right that there are only two myopic baselines and one non-myopic baseline, with rollout numbers copied from [1] (lines 292-293). It misreads lines 37-38, which limit the myopia claim to constrained methods and name exceptions. Reviewer_wxwg is right that the high-dimension claim (lines 65-67) has no experiment. Reviewer_F2Td called real-world experiments its "only concern", received one on 2021-08-11, and never edited its 6. Reviewer_iK1S (6, confidence 5) also never edited. The meta-review copies vkHd's phrase "1-3 real benchmarks ..., more baselines, and ablation studies". It names no rebuttal result and says no reviewer would champion the paper.

**Committee B (poster).** Ratings 7, 7, 6, 5. The three reviewers who raised spent 2 to 3 hours each. HPhn's review is short but informed: it calls the work incremental over Wu & Frazier 2019. It added a point for the "convincing responses and promising test case results". Strq's 7 rests on a thin review that found the estimator "a little bit hard to follow". uDRU misread the method. It says the method "utilizes reparameterization trick", but lines 18-19 say it does not. It says 2-OPT-C ignores the constraint, but line 164 has the feasibility term. Its text still says the paper "cannot be accepted in the current form", yet it rose to 6 for "the amount of work". Reviewer_8KpE wrote the most specific review (about 6 hours) and stayed at 5, with open questions on the portfolio set-up. The meta-review calls this a "consensus" and the portfolio example "convincing". That example had no error bars. The rebuttal also claimed 2-OPT-C "substantially outperforms" everywhere, while its own tables show ties with PESC and ADMMBO.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A asked for random search, Thompson sampling and the other cited constrained methods as baselines (iK1S, vkHd). Only A challenged the high-dimension claim (wxwg) and the novelty claim at lines 220-221 (iK1S). Only A raised equality constraints and failures (iK1S) and weak motivation (vkHd). Only B raised clarity and self-containedness (uDRU). Only B asked whether non-myopia causes the gain, whether a modified EIC could match it, and what happens with costly infeasible samples or δ > 0 (8KpE). Only B asked for links to other multi-step methods (HPhn, 8KpE). None of these decided either outcome. The decisive issue and the evidence were shared; the committees weighted the rebuttal differently.
