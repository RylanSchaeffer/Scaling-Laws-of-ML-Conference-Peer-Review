**Verdict.** Committee A accepted and Committee B rejected on mostly the same facts. All eight reviewers called the ImageNet results strong. All eight also noted overlap with earlier hybrids or with relative attention, and the authors agreed that relative attention is "NOT" their contribution. A's reviewers let the results outweigh novelty: Reviewer_VNGC kept a 7 while writing that the authors "did not address my concerns". In B, Reviewer_PojV and Reviewer_qHHe made novelty decisive and gave 5s. PojV declined to judge "solely in terms of results being SOTA". B also found one real fault that A missed. Reviewer_BJnY (4, confidence 5) showed that the abstract and introduction overstate SOTA, and the submission confirms it. Lines 57-58 claim NFNet's "best public record", but the cited NFNet paper reports 86.5% with SAM, and Table 4 shows CoAtNet-3 only tying NFNet-F5 at 86.0%. The authors conceded, and the camera-ready rewords both claims. The fault is in the wording, not in the results. B's negative case also held three factual errors that the reviewers never retracted. Neither area chair (AC) gave an own reading. The split comes mainly from reviewer draw: how much weight novelty gets against strong empirical results.

**Committee A (poster).** Ratings 7, 6, 7, 7, mean 6.75. No review was edited and no score moved. Reviewer_euiw wrote the most careful review. It caught that the text says V1 pre-trains better while Table 7 shows 53.0 for both, and the authors conceded. Reviewer_err4 named precedents (LambdaNet, HaloNet, SASA) and asked for throughput and code. The authors left the code request unanswered. Reviewer_3fnX gave a 7 at confidence 3 in four short paragraphs. It cites no table or number, its only criticism is novelty, and it posted nothing after the rebuttal. It is weak evidence of quality. No A reviewer questioned the SOTA wording; VNGC and 3fnX accept it. The meta-review says "The rebuttal addressed all of the reviewers concerns", which VNGC's own comment contradicts. The AC followed the scores.

**Committee B (reject).** Ratings 4, 5, 6, 5, mean 5.0. BJnY and qHHe edited only on their posting days, before the rebuttal. No score moved after it. BJnY wrote the most specific review in either committee. Two of its three SOTA points hold, and so do the uncited ConViT and the missing ImageNet-V2/ReaL results and error bars. Its claim that no relative-attention ablation exists is wrong: Table 6 is that ablation. PojV reports 15 hours but asks why Table 2 contradicts "Transformer layers generalize better". The paper claims the reverse (lines 43-44, 200-201). qHHe's closest NLP precedent (arXiv 2106.05505) appeared after the submission PDF was made on 27 May 2021. qHHe's ViL point holds: CoAtNet-0 (81.6%, 4.2B FLOPs) trails ViL-S (82.0%, 4.9B FLOPs). Reviewer_5AC9 (1.5 hours) reasoned as A did, with originality low and significance high, and gave the only 6. The AC cited "slightly negative ratings", novelty and "ambiguous claims", and followed the scores.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only B challenged the SOTA claims, cited ConViT, ViL and NLP precedents, asked for ImageNet-V2/ReaL and error bars, and questioned the training recipe against DeiT. Only B found that checklist item 3(a) claims code that was not supplied, and only B raised compute and environmental cost. Only A caught the Table 7 text error and asked for PVT and the ResNet-ViT hybrid in the comparisons. Both raised novelty, the classification-only scope, missing latency or throughput figures, and reproducibility.
