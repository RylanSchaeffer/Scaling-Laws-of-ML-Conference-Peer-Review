**Verdict**

The spotlight/poster split is an AC tier call on identical reviews. All seven reviewers rated the paper 7 ("Good paper, accept") at confidence 2 or 3. None argued for a highlight, none found an error, and none checked the proofs. Both committees saw a clear theory paper that extends Abbe and Sandon's batch-size-1 result to mini-batch SGD, with constructions that look nothing like practical deep learning. Neither meta-review gives a reason for its tier. A's AC wrote "All reviewers and the AC like the paper" and chose poster. B's AC wrote "a solid work worthy of acceptance", which argues for acceptance, not for a highlight, and chose spotlight. The records differ in scrutiny, not in verdict. A had four reviewers and about 20 hours of reviewing; B had three reviewers and 9 hours. All the substantive questions came from A, and the rebuttal settled them. No unresolved doubt in A explains the poster, and no stronger praise in B explains the spotlight.

**Committees**

Committee A (7, 7, 7, 7; poster). Reviewer_Pvqb (8 h) wrote the most critical review. It questioned the "PAC" label, the bit-valued inputs, the technical novelty over Abbe and Sandon, and whether Algorithm 1 works with a fresh mini-batch per round. The last point was a misreading. On a fresh batch, the prefix grows by a bit chosen with probability w1/w, which follows the correct conditional distribution. The camera-ready keeps this design. Pvqb moved from 6 to 7 after the rebuttal. Reviewer_Gdae (5 h) asked informed questions on the loss, on Theorem 1's step count, and on ρ against SQ tolerance, then "heartily" recommended acceptance. The authors never answered its pointer to Malach, Yehudai, Shalev-Shwartz and Shamir, and the camera-ready does not cite that paper. Reviewer_Sk3C (confidence 2, proofs not read) found "no practical significance" and "unrealistic" architectures. Reviewer_uqhv is four points of praise and two notation fixes. The two-sentence meta-review restates the consensus.

Committee B (7, 7, 7; spotlight). Reviewer_Gr4i (2 h) said it did not know the key related works and could not judge novelty or technical merit. It also calls ρ "the error due to clipping". The paper defines ρ as a rounding precision and says clipping "plays no role" in its constructions. Reviewer_wyJF (confidence 2) gave three sentences of assessment ("extends non-trivially") and line-level remarks. Reviewer_1153 (3 h) gave generic praise and called the literature review "thorough". The authors posted one general reply; only Gr4i acknowledged it ("opinion remains unchanged"). The three-sentence meta-review paraphrases the reviewers and accepts the "limitations to applications to the real world". It names only the SQ simulation, not the PAC results that the reviewers saw as the advance, and it gives no reason for a spotlight. I checked claims against a/paper.pdf, which is the camera-ready.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised four points from Pvqb: the Algorithm 1 batch question, the misuse of "PAC", the bit-valued inputs, and the novelty over Abbe and Sandon. A also raised the squared loss with binary labels, ρ against SQ tolerance, the uncited related work, and the undefined D^b. Only B raised a title broader than the results and extensions to momentum and variance reduction. B also questioned the claim that differentiable learning is distinct from ERM, and the late introduction of PAC and SQ. Both noted the limited real-world relevance. None of the A-only issues survived the rebuttal as a flaw, and A's meta-review cites none of them. At the reviewer level, both committees weighed the same facts the same way. Their ACs mapped a unanimous 7 to different tiers.
