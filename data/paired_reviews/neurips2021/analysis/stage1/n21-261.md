**Verdict.** The panels were nearly the same; the area chairs (ACs) made the difference. Committee A rated 7, 6, 6, 5 (mean 6.0); Committee B rated 6, 4, 6, 7 (mean 5.75). Each had three reviewers at 6 or above. Both questioned novelty against Zhao et al. A's AC called the rebuttal on Zhao et al. (2018) and Hoffman et al. (2018) "well addressed" and accepted. B's AC rejected against three positive reviews. It relied on its own comparison ("I'm very familiar with Zhao et al.") and on Reviewer_EJQZ's two post-rebuttal points. Both points hold in the paper. Theorem 3 is Theorem 1 with the target-mixture distance replaced by pairwise distances. Theorem 8 has the form of the Zhao et al. (2019) bound; lines 236 and 252 present it as a generalisation. A never discussed either point, but the paper states the second openly. The committees mostly weighed the same incremental theory against different bars. No fatal flaw separates them.

**Committee A (poster).** Reviewer_dgwj (7) found two typos and asked for comparison with Mansour et al. (2009) and Hoffman et al. (2018). The rebuttal made it "more convinced", and it kept 7. Reviewer_XYGp (6) asked how Theorem 1 compares with Zhao et al. (2018), reread the paper, and kept 6. Reviewer_kHSY (6) rewrites the abstract as its summary, praises the bounds in general terms, and puts its requests in the limitations field. It never replied. Reviewer_C95H (5) made the sharpest objection: the "first work" claim (lines 53-54) ignores Zhao et al. (2018), which the paper cites only inside a list (line 338). The AC's meta-review restates the abstract and calls the comparisons "well addressed". XYGp and dgwj agreed; C95H never answered the rebuttal or the authors' reminder.

**Committee B (reject).** Reviewer_EJQZ (4, six hours) is the only detractor. Its claims check out: Theorem 3 follows from Theorem 1, Theorem 8 extends Zhao et al. (2019), and Theorem 7 is a fixed-hypothesis bound without uniform convergence. The authors called the last point future work. Their answer on Zhao et al. (2019) said the JS divergence "becomes infinity" on disjoint supports. That is false, because JS is bounded by log 2. EJQZ kept 4 and named the two points that remained. Reviewer_ssg2 (7, confidence 3) called Theorem 8 "surprising" and wrote "Concerns addressed". Reviewer_RQ3Y (confidence 2) rose from 5 to 6 without a stated reason. Its figure concerns were right: the authors' 10-run rerun moves target accuracy only from 0.705 ± 0.005 to 0.712 ± 0.006. Reviewer_BYyp (6) wrote three short paragraphs. It praises "effectiveness" in a paper with no method and asks for a DANN comparison, though the experiments already train a DANN-style objective. It never replied. The AC credits EJQZ's points to "reviewers" and adds DRO and CORAL, which no reviewer named.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only B said Theorem 3 is a corollary of Theorem 1, Theorem 8 extends Zhao et al. (2019), and Theorem 7 ignores the empirical-population gap (EJQZ). Only B noted the single-run Figure 2a and that general DI helps only early in training in Figure 3a (RQ3Y); the figures confirm both. Only B questioned the infinite-capacity assumption (ssg2) and the lack of a new algorithm (AC). Only A asked about Hoffman et al. (2018) and Mansour et al. (2009) (dgwj, kHSY), Wu et al. (2019) relaxed alignment (C95H), and the choice of source mixture (XYGp). Both asked about Zhao et al. (2018), thin experiments, and when the assumptions hold. Neither noticed that the submitted Theorem 8 lacks square roots on its loss terms; the camera-ready adds them.
