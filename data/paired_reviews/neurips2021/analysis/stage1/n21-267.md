gap counted, and one review and two area chairs decided it. Committee A's mean of 6.67 rests on
Reviewer_zi5m's 9; without it, A's mean is 5.5, the same as B's. In A, Reviewer_k7tB gave a correct
counterexample to Theorem 3.3: with a 1-hop EXTRACT, nodes d and g get identical subgraphs, yet 1-WL

large neighbourhood. The authors' defence, that L is a hyperparameter, concedes the small-subgraph
case. A's area chair called it "some discrepancy over the usefulness of the theory". B's area chair
read the proofs and found the same gap, and also that shaDow-SAGE contains GraphSAGE only when EXTRACT returns "the full L-hop
neighborhood" (lines 183-185). The AC added two over-claims and rejected, while calling the results
"great". These points are correct on the text. They concern how the theory is framed, which a
revision can scope, and the meta-review ends by asking for better writing. Same facts, weighted
differently.
