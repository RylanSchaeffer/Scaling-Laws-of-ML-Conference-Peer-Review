**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster, for the same reason. The paper reaches literature-level verified error in 160 epochs instead of 2,000 to 3,200. On CIFAR-10 it gets 65.58% (best run 65.03%) against 66.62% for Xu et al. Every reviewer valued this speedup. No reviewer ended as a champion or a detractor: A gave 6, 7, 6, 7 (mean 6.5) and B gave 6, 5, 6 (mean 5.67). Rebuttals lifted two reviewers in A and one in B. B was cooler. It had no 7, Reviewer_gGDq (confidence 5) called the four-part combination "somewhat unprincipled", and B's AC called the paper borderline. A's two 7s found the same fixes natural, and A's AC named no weakness. So the committees weighted the same facts a little differently, and both stayed above the line. Both under-weighted one fact. IBP initialization, the one derived component, does not help alone (Table 4: 67.01% to 67.63%). Its derivation ignores BN, as the camera-ready concedes. At 160 epochs, full BN alone already gives 67.01%. The paper claims that BN finding too, so the poster result stands.

**Committees**

Committee A (6, 7, 6, 7). Reviewer_E79q (8 hours) and Reviewer_3WeK (confidence 4) found the fixes natural. E79q correctly computed that a five-value λ search would cost about 47,000 s (Table 3). The authors answered with a sweep and one fixed λ. 3WeK noted that the initialization gains less than Sec. 3 suggests, and Table 4 confirms it. Reviewer_Myoo said BN breaks IBP. That is wrong, because BN uses clean-data statistics (l.181-183). Myoo also said that the Sec. 3.2.1 analysis ignores BN, and that BN may help optimization rather than ReLU balance. Both points are right, and Figure 2 supports the second. Myoo rose to 6 for a new BN ablation but stayed unsatisfied with the theory. Reviewer_e75G read Figure 5's verified error as "classification error" and called the TinyImageNet results too weak. The Table 2 caption gives the literature best as 84.14%. e75G rose to 6 after the clarification. The AC wrote three sentences, called the improvement "substantial" and named no weakness.

Committee B (6, 5, 6). Reviewer_NvoX wrote a short review with correct points. Figure 2 shows that BN alone barely changes ReLU balance (about 6% to 7% active). Table 4 lacks a row with all components but BN, and Table 2 lacks standard deviations. NvoX kept 6. Reviewer_gGDq liked the speedup but found the method unprincipled. gGDq correctly called "significantly outperform literature SOTA" (l.250) an overclaim: the margins are 1 to 2 points, from best runs. Reviewer_3ihN began at 3 or lower, mostly for notation. The submission gives W_i only a zero mean and a variance (l.129-130), not the symmetry that Appendix D needs. The camera-ready adds it. Clarifications took 3ihN to 4, and a longer-training plot took 3ihN to 5. The AC put the empirical gains above the missing theory, because "having principled approaches in this area is in the AC's opinion difficult".
