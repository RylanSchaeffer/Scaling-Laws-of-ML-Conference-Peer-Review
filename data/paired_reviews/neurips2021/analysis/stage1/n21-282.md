**Verdict**

Both committees rejected the paper for the same reason. Each judged the analysis correct and publishable, but too narrow or too incremental for NeurIPS. Raw scores leaned positive in both: A gave 7, 6, 4 (mean 5.67), and B gave 7, 3, 7, 6 (mean 5.75). The real support was thinner. A's 6 (Reviewer_9me5) restates the theorems and admits the reviewer has not followed DP research for years. B's second 7 (Reviewer_ghaZ) is generic praise at confidence 1. Both ACs turned a caveat from their most expert positive reviewer into the reason to reject. In A, Reviewer_ZYMd (7, confidence 5) wrote that "the contribution is somewhat narrow". In B, Reviewer_g7yk (6) called the work updated calculations for a well-known idea and doubted that it meets the NeurIPS bar. Neither AC rested the decision on its detractor. No score moved after the rebuttal. Both committees weighed the same facts the same way.

**Committees**

Committee A (7, 6, 4). ZYMd's review is short but specific. It notes that DNZMR17 already gives (0, δ)-DP for the Beta posterior. The paper omits this (l.70-73), and the authors conceded it. Reviewer_pz6h (4, eight hours) wrote the strongest technical critique, and the paper confirms it. Figure 1 shows weak guarantees: with α = 10, ε is near 5 at δ = 10^-8. The Section 4.1 bound (Eq. 10) is an explicit function of α' − α. The reply offered ε just above 4 at δ = 10^-8 as acceptable, and pz6h did not answer. The AC agreed with ZYMd that the results are correct. It then rejected for "the narrow scope of the problem" and "the extremely high bar".

Committee B (7, 3, 7, 6). Reviewer_Qa5w (7) praised the clarity, saw limited use cases, and kept its score. Reviewer_QwTp (3) wrote line-level comments on wording, and the authors accepted several fixes. Two of its claims are wrong. Lines 180-186 and 274-277 discuss limitations, including the case where the Gaussian mechanism needs fewer samples. Section 1.2 contrasts each prior result. A 3 for points that "individually can probably be easily solved" is out of proportion. g7yk supplied the decisive view: sound and worth publishing, perhaps in a journal. The AC echoed g7yk and called QwTp's points addressable. It added that the KL ordering in Theorem 3 lacks a justification. The rebuttal to Qa5w gives only the closed form as the reason, so this point holds.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised these points:

- weak (ε, δ) values at the plotted priors (pz6h; Figure 1 confirms);
- the uncited (0, δ)-DP result for Beta, and the link to prior trimming (ZYMd);
- ℓ∞ rather than ℓ1 error for normalized histograms (pz6h);
- confusing γ and δ notation in Theorem 2 (ZYMd).

Only B raised these points:

- low conceptual novelty (g7yk, AC), where ZYMd instead called the problem long open;
- imprecise wording and no explicit problem statement (QwTp);
- a single Gaussian baseline (QwTp; correct);
- "accuracy" for an error in Figure 2, and a stray α' in the proof of Theorem 1 (Qa5w, QwTp; both correct);
- the unjustified KL ordering (AC).

Both committees raised narrow applicability, a main result that is hard to read (pz6h; g7yk asks for an RDP statement), and an unclear role for Section 4.1 (pz6h; Qa5w). No one-sided issue changed a decision.
