**Verdict.** Committee B rejected because two of its reviewers checked the theory and found specific limits that Committee A's reviewers never examined. Reviewer_y5UR showed that the main bound does not improve with more rounds. Theorem 4 and Table 1 carry a σB/√(MK) term, where Local SGD and FedAc have σB/√(MKR). The authors conceded this. Reviewer_yNcz showed that the headline claim, beating the first-order lower bound, needs H=B=σ=α=1, M→∞ and ρ below about 1/log K. Lines 219-222 use exactly that example, and the authors did not answer. The camera-ready later weakens "less than the lower bound" to "matches the lower bound" and concedes that FedAc is better for large R. B also found real notation defects. Committee A noted regime-bound gains and strong assumptions only in general terms, and judged them normal for a theory paper. No A reviewer noted the R-dependence, or that the experiments run FedSN-Lite instead of the analysed FedSN. B's case also has weak parts. Its AC's "poor performance" is false for Figures 1-2, where FedSN-Lite is best or tied, and "inconsistency between theory and experiments" overstates a loose upper bound. The split comes from depth of scrutiny and from different weight on a narrow-regime theoretical gain.
