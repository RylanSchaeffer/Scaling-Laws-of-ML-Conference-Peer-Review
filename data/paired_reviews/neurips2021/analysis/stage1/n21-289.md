**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster with the same mean rating, 6.75. All eight final ratings are 6 or 7, so there is no discrepancy to explain. Neither committee had a champion. Each had one sceptic who moved to 6 on promises for the camera-ready. The committees probed different halves of the paper. A probed the experiments and found the OOD evidence qualitative. B probed the theory and found that the triangle-wave and periodic-ReLU results hold only approximately. Both flaws were real and repairable. The sinusoidal result is exact, and the dropped harmonics of the piecewise-linear waves carry about 1.4% of the prior variance (my calculation, 1 − 96/π⁴). Both ACs followed the consensus and overstated what the rebuttal delivered. Nobody in A remarked that the rebuttal's own AUROC numbers show no advantage of global over local stationarity, the paper's distinctive empirical claim.

**Committees**

Committee A (7, 6, 7, 7). All four reviewers chose confidence levels whose label says the math was not checked, and none discussed the derivations. Reviewer_pm5N (8 hours) called the quantitative OOD evaluation "too thin". pm5N also noted that local and global stationarity perform equally on rotated MNIST, which l.267-269 confirm. Reviewer_KDY4 asked for AUROC or AUPR on CIFAR-10. The authors gave AUROC against SVHN (ReLU 96.1, stationary models 98.1 to 98.8) and promised more. pm5N was "quite satisfied" and rose to 6. But those numbers put global and local level (RBF 98.8 against 98.7; Matérn-3/2 98.1 against 98.3), which supports pm5N's own objection. Reviewer_L92o (3 hours, confidence 2) wrote a descriptive review. Its one question, on training difficulty, is answered at l.300-302. The AC's four sentences defer to the reviewers and claim "many of the reviewers raised their scores". Only pm5N's review changed after the rebuttal.

Committee B (7, 7, 6, 7). Reviewer_C4fJ (confidence 4) showed that the appendix keeps only the first Fourier harmonic for the triangle wave and periodic ReLU, while the main text claims "a direct correspondence" (l.207-218). The point is correct: the authors conceded it, and the camera-ready calls the link "approximate". C4fJ's other charges are wrong. The paper discusses limitations (l.300-305) and cites Matthews et al. as [7]. The "not original" reference is the cited work [31], whose activations are locally stationary and not periodic. C4fJ stayed below acceptance until the authors promised to "aim to quantify" the error, then moved to 6. The review text still ends "I don't recommend acceptance". Reviewer_8dcJ found missing limit arguments in the same proofs and no NTK discussion; the authors conceded both. Reviewer_6Fuy (2 hours) said the experiments do not use the Student-t/Matérn link, but Fig. 1, Table 1 and Fig. 6 do. The AC says the authors resolved the issue by "adding error estimates", which they had only promised.

**Issues raised by one committee only**

Only A raised the missing OOD metrics and OOD baselines (KDY4, pm5N), equal local and global results on rotated MNIST, the choice of UCI data sets (pm5N), MAP against Bayesian inference (S2q4) and training difficulty (L92o). Only B raised these issues:

- the truncated Fourier series behind the triangle-wave and periodic-ReLU results (C4fJ);
- proof rigour in App. A.3 and A.4 (8dcJ);
- finite-width error of the periodic models, worse under heavy-tailed priors (8dcJ, C4fJ);
- the missing NTK and infinite-width literature (8dcJ, C4fJ);
- originality against Meronen et al. 2020 (C4fJ).

Both raised the small NLPD margins in Table 1 (pm5N, qVhs) and the single-model Fig. 6 (pm5N, qVhs). None of these issues changed a decision.
