**Verdict.** Committee A accepted and Committee B rejected on the same facts. No reviewer disputed novelty or technical soundness. Reviewers in both committees found the presentation abstract and the experiments small. Before the rebuttal the committees stood close: A at 5, 5 or lower, 7 and 7; B at 5, 5 and at least 7. Two things split them. A's critics, Reviewer_Ae4v and Reviewer_3PA6, asked mostly clarity questions. Explanations answered them, and each raised one point to 6. B's critics, Reviewer_PZ8J and Reviewer_4J52, asked for new work: AIS or HMC baselines, higher-dimensional real tasks, a bias study and a focused framing. A rebuttal can only promise such work, and both held at 5. Then B's only champion, Reviewer_UVCx, read the other reviews and lowered to 6. A's two 7s carry the least support, and neither reviewer joined the discussion. The submission supports B's empirical points. The conclusion (lines 305-307) claims that NVI outperforms baselines in all three settings. But Figure 6 shows parity with the hand-coded GMM heuristic (log Ẑ −433 vs −437), and Section 4.3 has no quantitative comparison. Ae4v saw this weakness too and still accepted on technical merit. Each area chair (AC) followed its committee. The split is a difference in weight, made larger by reviewer draw.

**Committee A (poster).** Ratings 6, 6, 7, 7, mean 6.5. Ae4v wrote A's most careful review. It called the experiments "not particularly compelling" and the annealing task "quite toy". It also found that Section 4.3 has no quantitative results and cites a "Figure 9" that is not in the paper. The submission confirms both points. Reviewer_nv8j gave a 7 on 79 words with no specific point and called the paper "a clear accept". That review is no evidence of quality. Reviewer_FQ8n gave a 7 at confidence 2 and is "not familiar with the SMC literature". It called the claims "well-supported", but Figure 6 and Section 4.3 do not support them. The meta-review repeats "solid and novel technical contribution" and lists only presentation fixes. It omits the experimental critique. The AC followed the scores.

**Committee B (reject).** Ratings 5, 5, 6, mean 5.33, at confidences 4, 3 and 4. PZ8J wrote B's most specific review, and each checked point holds. The annealing target is 2-D, and the HMM data are simulated. No AIS or MCMC-kernel baseline appears. The self-normalized gradients are biased, and no experiment measures the bias. 4J52 called the baselines "idiosyncratic", and footnote 2 agrees: "AVO-flow is not technically a baseline". UVCx correctly found near-ties in Table 1 without error bars. The authors promised an AIS baseline, a non-synthetic dataset, a bias study and an ASVI baseline. 4J52 conceded that the structured-VI papers are "orthogonal" but kept the focus objection. The authors' last reply "emphatically" disagreed and drew no answer. UVCx lowered on the other reviewers' arguments, not on new evidence. The AC summarized PZ8J and 4J52 and rejected.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A asked for background on nested importance sampling, an algorithm box, implementation details and speed comparisons. Only A noted that Section 4.3 has no quantitative results. Only B asked for AIS or HMC baselines, a bias analysis, high-dimensional real tasks and confidence intervals. Only B called the state-space experiment off scope and asked for structured-VI baselines. Both raised the abstract presentation, the toy scale, small margins over AVO, the relation to the ELBO, full optimization failing in Section 4.2, and thin limitations.
