**Verdict**

Both committees recommended a poster, and the agreement is robust. No reviewer in either committee found a technical error, and no rating is above 7. So neither committee had a case for a higher tier. The committees differed in how strictly they judged the writing, not in outcome. In A, the only sub-threshold rating (Reviewer_q1b7, raised to 5) attacked the presentation alone: "the formal results are good, but the paper needs a thorough rewriting". The submission confirms the core of this. Query complexity is defined informally (l.97-99), "nested union" is undefined (l.43), and no figure shows rays, extreme vertices or the Radon number. A's AC made the rewrite and Reviewer_8BJz's polynomial-time answers conditions for the camera-ready. In B, Reviewer_KdHb and Reviewer_sgyY called the paper well written. B's doubts were about significance and novelty, and the rebuttal lifted Reviewer_G7Y8 to 6 and Reviewer_mSJZ to 7. So A weighed the same dense presentation more heavily. This explains its lower mean (6.25 against 6.75), but the difference did not reach the decision. B's record is thinner: 1,361 review words against 2,320, and a two-sentence meta-review. But B's two careful reviewers (KdHb; mSJZ, who checked proofs) also gave 7.

**Committees**

Committee A (5, 6, 7, 7). Reviewer_q1b7 wrote the longest review (986 words), and parts of it overreach. The introduction names active learning on graphs in its first sentence (l.13-16). The suggested k log ℓ count for hypergrids is wrong, because one cut edge fixes every label there. h(G) does not depend on the labels (l.78-79). The "plagiarism" hint concerns MLG 2020, a non-archival workshop paper by the same authors, which the camera-ready cites. The AC told the reviewers to disregard it. Reviewer_8BJz asked for a restructure and figures. After a reviewer-AC discussion, 8BJz asked whether Theorem 6 is reachable in polynomial time. The authors conceded that this needs a computable minimum hull set, which is APX-hard in general. 8BJz then accepted on condition that these answers enter the paper. Reviewer_1KsL and Reviewer_SPWf wrote short, accurate reviews and did not join the discussion. The meta-review cites the majority and lists the revisions.

Committee B (7, 6, 7, 7; confidences 3, 3, 3, 2). Reviewer_KdHb gave an accurate summary; Table 1 and Theorem 10 match its tightness claims. KdHb also noted that convex communities (Table 2) do not imply halfspace-separable labels, and the authors conceded. Reviewer_mSJZ (16 hours, confidence 2) checked the proofs up to Theorem 10. This is the only proof check in either committee. mSJZ rose to 7 on the authors' novelty list. Reviewer_G7Y8 wrote 92 words and asked whether the results recover Euclidean halfspace bounds; l.222-227 already say they do not transfer. G7Y8 rose to 6 after a list of applications. Reviewer_sgyY spent 2 hours, did not check proofs and did not reply. The AC wrote two sentences and cited unanimity.
