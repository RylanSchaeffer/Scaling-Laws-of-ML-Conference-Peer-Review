**Verdict.** Both committees saw the same paper and the same weakness, but they weighted it differently. All seven reviewers endorsed the method, an exact and learnable parameterization of GENERIC brackets. Five found the experiments thin. The paper has two toy benchmarks whose observed states have one degree of freedom. Its only baselines are a neural ODE (NODE) and a penalty variant. In A, explanatory rebuttals raised two scores. The area chair (AC) then turned 7/6/8 into a spotlight and gave no reason for the higher tier. In B, Reviewer_xSwS rated 3 for the thin experiments and did not move. B's two-sentence meta-review rejected on the detractor's "insufficient empirical evidence". The paper supports the shared doubt. The gas-container figure shows the best of five runs, with no spread. No table reports an error. The text calls the Figure 3 standard deviations "good agreement", but the caption puts GNODE 6 to 10 times below the analytic baseline. Neither committee caught that. The record supports B's doubts about the evidence more than A's spotlight.

**Committee A (spotlight).** Ratings 7, 6, 8 (mean 7.0); reviewers spent 4.7 hours on average. Reviewer_hnow first rated the work "borderline" because Sections 4.1–4.2 reproduce Öttinger's brackets [42]. The paper itself says so (lines 103–104). The authors answered that the learnable symmetrizations are new. hnow checked [42], agreed, and raised to 7. Reviewer_DqnY listed a rank-1 D, unexplained network sizes, one penalty weight with no rationale, and code claimed in the checklist but not supplied. The paper confirms each point. DqnY raised to 6 on promised appendices and still called the comparisons "a little weak". Reviewer_PQY3 gave 8 with a solid review and did not join the discussion. The AC wrote "a very good paper and a clear accept" and conceded the experiments "can be further improved". Nothing in the record argues for a spotlight.

**Committee B (reject).** Recorded ratings 3, 6, 5, 5 (mean 4.75); reviewers spent 3.6 hours on average. Reviewer_AB4a declared a raise to 7 on 2021-08-25, but the form still shows 5. The AC counted the 7, so the final positions were 3/6/7/5 (mean 5.25). The factual points of xSwS hold: two low-dimensional benchmarks, one existing-ML baseline, and no spread on the gas result. Its writing complaint is weak. The paper declares the Einstein convention (lines 104–105), and all six other reviewers praised the clarity. The authors misread a question from AB4a twice, and AB4a dropped 6 to 5. Three high-dimensional GENERIC examples then won the 7. Reviewer_DeZu cut 7 to 6, citing weaknesses "other reviewers have noted", and offered to return to 7. The authors never replied. Reviewer_D146 gave 5 after 2 hours on unsupported claims ("learning entropy growth is not as exciting") and never replied. The AC stated no view of its own.

**Issues one committee raised and the other did not.** Only A questioned novelty against Öttinger, and the rebuttal settled it. Only A probed the tuning of the penalty baseline, the rank of D and the missing code. Only B pressed the writing for an ML audience and the scope of the degeneracy condition in high dimensions. Only B asked about the O(N^3) and O(N^4) tensor cost, exactness under dopri5, and the unclear Section 5.4. Both raised the toy scale, the weak baselines, the missing gas-container spread and the duplicate reference [21]/[36]. The camera-ready still shows the best of five gas runs, though the authors promised error bars to both committees.
